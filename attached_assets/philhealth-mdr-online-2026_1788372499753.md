## PhilHealth MDR Online: How to View, Download, and Print It

You can get your **PhilHealth MDR online** through the official PhilHealth Member Portal — no branch visit needed for a standard copy. This guide is for any PhilHealth member (employed, self-employed, voluntary, or OFW) who needs their Member Data Record for a hospital admission, an employer requirement, or simply to confirm their membership details are correct. It covers what the MDR actually is, how to get it online in 2026, and what to do if the information on it is wrong.

### What Is the PhilHealth MDR?

The **Member Data Record (MDR)** is PhilHealth's own summary of your membership: it typically shows your PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN), your name, your membership category and status, and the dependents currently listed under your account. Hospitals and PhilHealth-accredited facilities commonly ask for a recent MDR when processing an admission or a claim, since it confirms that you (and any dependents involved) are recognized in PhilHealth's system.

**MDR vs. PhilHealth ID — these are not the same thing:**

| | Member Data Record (MDR) | PhilHealth ID |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | A record document with your membership and dependent details | An identification card/number |
| Format | Printable PDF | Physical or digital card |
| What it proves | Active membership status and who your dependents are | Who you are as a PhilHealth member |
| Where hospitals look first | Typically the MDR | Usually alongside a valid ID, not instead of the MDR |

### Who Can Get an MDR Online?

Any registered PhilHealth member with an activated Member Portal account can view and print their own MDR. This includes employed members, self-employed/voluntary members, and OFWs. According to PhilHealth's own Online Services listing, the Member Portal is built specifically to let members "access [their] PhilHealth records, contributions, and MDR online" and to "view or print MDR" — this is an official, intended function of the portal, not a workaround.

### How to Get Your PhilHealth MDR Online (Step-by-Step)

1. **Go to the official Member Portal.** Visit memberinquiry.philhealth.gov.ph. You can also reach it through philhealth.gov.ph under "Online Services." Always confirm you're on the official philhealth.gov.ph domain before entering any personal details.
2. **Log in or create an account.** If you already have a Member Portal account, log in with your 12-digit PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN), your password, and the captcha shown. If you don't have an account yet, register first, then activate it through the confirmation link sent to your email.
3. **Open your Member Information.** Once logged in, your membership details typically display automatically on your dashboard.
4. **Click "Print MDR."** This generates a PDF version of your current Member Data Record.
5. **Save or print the PDF.** Download it to your phone or computer, and print a physical copy if you'll need it somewhere without reliable internet access, such as a hospital admissions desk.
6. **Double-check the details.** Before using the printed or downloaded copy for any transaction, review your name, membership status, and listed dependents for accuracy.

### If the Online Method Doesn't Work

Government portals occasionally run into technical issues. If you can't access or generate your MDR online, you can request a copy in person at any PhilHealth Local Health Insurance Office (LHIO) instead.

### Correcting Errors on Your MDR

The Member Portal is built for *viewing and printing* your MDR — it is not where you correct wrong information on it. If your MDR shows an incorrect name, a missing or wrong dependent, or another data error, PhilHealth's own published amendment procedure (used for both informal-economy and lifetime members) is:

1. **Download the PhilHealth Member Registration Form (PMRF)** from philhealth.gov.ph.
2. **Tick "For Updating"** in the upper-right corner of the form.
3. **Fill out the PMRF** with the corrected or additional information.
4. **Submit the properly filled-out PMRF to the nearest PhilHealth Office**, along with supporting documents (for example, PSA-issued civil registry documents establishing your relationship to a dependent, or a valid government ID for a name correction).
5. **Await the printout of your updated MDR**, per PhilHealth's official amendment procedure.

PhilHealth's published guidance describes this correction path through a PhilHealth Office rather than through the Member Portal itself. If you've seen other guides describe emailing scanned PMRF documents to a local office as an alternative, treat that as something to confirm directly with your nearest PhilHealth office, since it isn't part of PhilHealth's own published step-by-step amendment procedure referenced above.

### Common Problems

- **"Account not found" when logging in.** Having a PhilHealth PIN does not automatically mean you have a Member Portal account — these are separate. You may need to complete portal registration first.
- **MDR doesn't reflect a recent update.** After submitting a correction (like a new dependent), it can take some time before the change appears. Don't assume the update is finished until you print a fresh MDR and see the change reflected.
- **Portal is down or won't load.** This is a known limitation of online government systems generally. If it happens, request your MDR in person at a PhilHealth Local Health Insurance Office instead.
- **Confusing the MDR with the PhilHealth ID.** Hospitals typically ask specifically for the MDR, not just the ID card — bring the MDR even if you already have your ID with you.

### What to Do If Something Goes Wrong

- **Wrong personal details or missing dependent:** Follow the official PMRF-based amendment procedure above rather than assuming the portal will let you self-correct it.
- **Urgent need before a hospital admission:** Try the Member Portal first since it's usually the fastest option; if it fails, go to the nearest PhilHealth Local Health Insurance Office in person.
- **Unresolved discrepancy after an update:** Follow up directly with PhilHealth through its official channels (verify current contact details on philhealth.gov.ph, since published hotline and email details can change) rather than relying on third-party listings.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Is getting my MDR online free?**
Yes. Viewing and printing your MDR through the official Member Portal is a standard, no-cost member service.

**Can I use a screenshot of my MDR instead of printing it?**
The guidance available describes downloading/printing a PDF copy; the article does not have a verified official source confirming whether a screenshot alone is accepted everywhere, so it's best to have the actual downloaded PDF on hand (or a printed copy) and confirm acceptable formats with the specific facility if you're unsure.

**Do I need to renew or reprint my MDR regularly?**
There's no verified official rule requiring a fixed reprint schedule; however, since hospitals generally want a document that reflects your *current* status and dependents, it's practical to print a fresh copy whenever your details change or before an admission, rather than relying on an old printout.

**Can I add a dependent to my MDR entirely online?**
Based on PhilHealth's own published amendment procedure, the confirmed path is submitting a completed PMRF (marked "For Updating") to a PhilHealth Office, after which you receive an updated MDR. This article did not find an official PhilHealth source confirming a fully online, portal-based dependent-addition process, so don't assume one exists without checking directly with PhilHealth.

**What if I forgot my PhilHealth number (PIN)?**
This falls outside what could be verified for this guide. Contact PhilHealth directly through its official channels or visit a Local Health Insurance Office with a valid ID for assistance.
