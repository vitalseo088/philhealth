## PhilHealth Portal Registration: How to Create Your Member Portal Account (2026 Guide)

If you already have a PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN), PhilHealth portal registration means creating your **Member Portal account** at the official portal, `memberinquiry.philhealth.gov.ph`. If you don't have a PIN yet, you need to complete the **online PIN application** first — that's a separate step, and this guide walks through both.

This guide is for individual members — employed, self-employed, voluntary members, and OFWs — who want to register on the official PhilHealth Member Portal in 2026. It does not cover the employer portal (EPRS) or the healthcare-provider portal (IHCP), which use separate registration systems.

## Quick Answer

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| You already have a 12-digit PhilHealth PIN | Go to the Member Portal login page and click **Create Account** to register for online access. |
| You do not have a PIN yet | Use the **PhilHealth Member Registration (PIN Application)** facility on the same portal to apply for a PIN first. |
| You forgot your portal password | Use **Forgot Password** on the login page. |
| You want to check your PhilHealth number/status only | Use the separate **PhilHealth Check** facility. |

**Official portal:** `https://memberinquiry.philhealth.gov.ph/member/login.xhtml`

Always type this address directly or reach it through the official www.philhealth.gov.ph homepage. PhilHealth's own website carries scam warnings, since fake "PhilHealth portal" pages have circulated online — never enter your PIN, password, or personal documents on a site that isn't on the philhealth.gov.ph domain.

## What Is the PhilHealth Member Portal?

The PhilHealth Member Portal is the official online facility of the Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth) that lets individual members access their own records without visiting a branch. According to PhilHealth's official website, the portal allows members to access their records and contributions, make online premium payments, view or print their Member Data Record (MDR), and select a YAKAP clinic.

This isn't a brand-new service. PhilHealth first launched a "Member Inquiry" facility in 2015 to let members verify their profile details online, and it later relaunched a modernized version — the current Member Portal — to expand what members can do from home, including downloading and printing the MDR. "PhilHealth portal registration" today refers to signing up for this current version.

## Who Needs to Register?

You can create a Member Portal account if you are an individual PhilHealth member, including:

- Employed members (private or government sector)
- Self-employed and voluntary members
- Overseas Filipino Workers (OFWs)
- Other individual member categories recognized by PhilHealth

You need a valid **PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN)** before you can create a Member Portal account. If you've never registered with PhilHealth before and don't have a PIN, you'll need to apply for one first — see the section below.

## Before You Start: What You'll Need

Based on the official Member Portal account creation form, you'll be asked to provide:

- Your 12-digit **PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN)**
- Your **last name** and **first name** exactly as recorded with PhilHealth
- Your **date of birth**
- Your **sex** (male/female, as recorded)
- A valid, active **email address**
- Your **mobile number**
- A **password** that you create for the portal (see requirements below)

**Password requirements** (per the official account creation form):

- 8–32 characters
- At least one digit (0–9)
- At least one uppercase letter
- At least one lowercase letter
- At least one special character (e.g., `! @ # $ % ^ & *`)

You'll also need to complete a CAPTCHA to submit the form.

## Step-by-Step: Registering on the PhilHealth Portal (You Already Have a PIN)

1. **Go to the official Member Portal.**
   Visit `memberinquiry.philhealth.gov.ph/member/login.xhtml`, or reach it through the "Online Services" / Member Portal link on www.philhealth.gov.ph.

2. **Click "Create Account."**
   This link sits on the login page, alongside "Forgot Password" and the separate PIN Application link.

3. **Fill out the Member Portal Account Creation form.**
   Enter your PIN, last name, first name, date of birth, sex, email address, and mobile number exactly as they match your PhilHealth records. Mismatched details are one of the most common reasons registration fails.

4. **Create your password.**
   Set a password that meets the requirements above, and confirm it in the second field.

5. **Complete the CAPTCHA and submit.**
   Click **Create Account** to submit the form.

6. **Check your email.**
   The email address you provide is used for account-related notices. If you don't see a confirmation after submitting, check your spam or junk folder before trying again. If you're unsure whether your account was created successfully, try logging in directly at the login page using your PIN and the password you just set, or contact PhilHealth for assistance rather than resubmitting the form multiple times.

7. **Log in.**
   Once your account is active, return to the login page, enter your PIN and password, and complete the CAPTCHA to access your dashboard.

## Step-by-Step: Applying for a PIN First (You Don't Have One Yet)

If you have never been registered with PhilHealth, you cannot create a Member Portal account yet — you need a PIN first. According to the official PIN Application page on the Member Portal:

1. **Go to the "PhilHealth Member Registration" (PIN Application) link** on the Member Portal login page.
2. **Enter a valid email address** and complete the CAPTCHA to proceed. PhilHealth's official instructions state that this email address can only be used once for a PIN application, and that a registrant cannot submit more than one application.
3. **Complete the online PhilHealth Member Registration Form (PMRF).**
4. **Upload a supporting document**, such as a birth certificate or another valid, government-issued ID. The official instructions state that the application will be disregarded if the required supporting document is not uploaded.
5. **Submit your application.**
   PhilHealth's official page states that electronically submitted registrations with complete supporting documents are processed within **three (3) to five (5) working days**, and that your PIN will be sent to the email address you provided once processing is complete.
6. **Create your Member Portal account** using your new PIN once you receive it, following the steps in the section above.

## Common Problems and What to Do

**"My details don't match, and the system won't let me register."**
Your last name, first name, date of birth, and PIN must match what's on file with PhilHealth exactly. If you've had a legal name change or believe your PhilHealth records contain an error, this typically needs to be corrected through PhilHealth directly (such as through an updated PMRF), rather than by adjusting what you type on the registration form.

**"I didn't get my PIN after applying."**
Official instructions note that complete applications are processed within 3 to 5 working days, with the PIN sent by email. If it's been longer than that, check your spam folder first. If you still don't see it, it's best to follow up with PhilHealth directly rather than resubmitting a new application, since the official process does not allow more than one PIN application per person.

**"I can't log in even though I registered."**
Double-check that you're on the correct official URL and not a lookalike site. If your password isn't working, use "Forgot Password" on the login page rather than repeatedly guessing, which can lock the account.

**"I forgot my PhilHealth PIN entirely."**
The Member Portal login page also links to a separate "PhilHealth Check" facility, which is designed for checking PhilHealth information; if you're unable to recover your PIN this way, contact PhilHealth's official support channels for identity verification.

## What to Do If Something Goes Wrong

- **Don't create multiple accounts or PIN applications** hoping one will work — PhilHealth's own instructions say only one PIN application is allowed per person, and duplicate attempts can complicate verification.
- **Never pay anyone to "register" or "activate" your PhilHealth portal account.** PhilHealth's website carries active scam warnings; treat any request for payment tied to portal registration as suspicious.
- **Use only the official domain.** Confirm the address reads `philhealth.gov.ph` (or its `memberinquiry.philhealth.gov.ph` subdomain) before entering any personal information.
- **If you suspect a scam or phishing attempt**, or if your registration issue isn't resolved through the portal, contact PhilHealth through its official channels listed on www.philhealth.gov.ph rather than a number or link found elsewhere online.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is PhilHealth portal registration free?**
The official Member Portal is a government member service; PhilHealth's website separately warns members to be alert to scams, which is a strong signal that no legitimate registration step should require payment. If anyone asks you to pay to register or activate your account, do not proceed and report it to PhilHealth.

**Do I need a PIN before I can register on the portal?**
Yes. The Member Portal account creation form requires a PIN. If you don't have one, use the PIN Application (PMRF) facility on the same portal first.

**Can I register on the portal using my mobile phone?**
The Member Portal is accessible through any device with an internet connection and a browser, based on how PhilHealth describes the facility, though this guide does not verify performance on every device or browser.

**What's the difference between the Member Portal and "PhilHealth Check"?**
Both are linked from the same login page, but they serve different purposes: the Member Portal is where you register for and access your personal account and records, while PhilHealth Check is a separate facility for checking PhilHealth information. This guide covers Member Portal registration specifically.

**How long does portal account creation take?**
The official account creation page does not publish a specific processing time for portal account activation itself. This is separate from PIN applications for new members, which PhilHealth states are processed in 3 to 5 working days.

**What if my registration issue isn't covered here?**
For anything not explicitly confirmed by PhilHealth's official materials — such as edge cases in document requirements for specific member categories — contact PhilHealth directly rather than relying on unofficial guides, since requirements can vary by member type and may change.
