# PhilHealth YAKAP Consultation: How It Works and How to Book One (2026 Guide)

A PhilHealth YAKAP consultation is your visit with a doctor (or other qualified health worker) at a YAKAP Clinic you've registered with — and it's the entry point to everything else YAKAP offers, including free lab tests, medicines, and cancer screening referrals. This guide walks through what the consultation actually involves, who can get one, and exactly how to book it.

This guide is for PhilHealth members and dependents who want to use their YAKAP primary care benefit in 2026. It reflects PhilHealth's current YAKAP framework, which replaced the earlier Konsulta program and is governed mainly by PhilHealth Circular No. 2025-0017 (selection and empanelment rules) and the official YAKAP FAQ. Some clinic-level details — such as appointment scheduling and exact wait times — vary by clinic and aren't set out in PhilHealth's national issuances, so this guide notes where that's the case.

## What Is a PhilHealth YAKAP Consultation?

**YAKAP** stands for **Yaman ng Kalusugan Program**, PhilHealth's primary care benefit package. It replaced and expanded the earlier **Konsulta** program, adding a broader medicine benefit and cancer screening coverage on top of the consultations Konsulta already provided.

A "consultation" under YAKAP is simply a visit to your **registered YAKAP Clinic**, where a PhilHealth-accredited primary care physician (or other qualified provider) assesses your health, reviews your history and risk factors, and decides what care you need next — whether that's a follow-up visit, a lab test, a prescription, or a referral for something like cancer screening.

Under YAKAP, based on clinical need and the doctor's recommendation, a member and their registered dependents can access:

- Consultations and check-ups at their registered YAKAP Clinic
- Outpatient laboratory tests
- Selected cancer screening tests
- Free essential medicines (up to ₱20,000 worth per beneficiary per year) through accredited GAMOT pharmacies

The consultation is the starting point for all of these — you generally can't access the other benefits without first being seen at your registered clinic.

## Who Is Eligible for a YAKAP Consultation?

PhilHealth's official FAQ states that YAKAP is intended for every Filipino — you simply need to register with a YAKAP Clinic to use the free benefits and services.

A few registration rules to be aware of, based on PhilHealth Circular No. 2025-0017:

- **Beneficiaries 18 years old and above** may register, select their clinic, and sign agreements independently, without needing parental or guardian consent.
- **Minor beneficiaries** must have all their official YAKAP transactions — including registration, clinic selection, and signing the Mutual Care Agreement — handled by a parent or legal guardian, since minors don't have legal personality to transact on their own under this circular.
- Both members and their qualified dependents are eligible, but each person is registered and empaneled separately — a dependent can be registered at the same clinic as the member or at a different, more convenient one.

The circular also establishes a geographic-proximity consideration in clinic assignment, intended to ensure the beneficiary can realistically and regularly reach their chosen YAKAP Clinic. PhilHealth's issuances do not spell out an exact distance figure in the material reviewed for this article, so members should confirm any distance-related limits directly with PhilHealth or their Local Health Insurance Office (LHIO) if this affects their clinic choice.

## How to Register and Book Your First YAKAP Consultation

Registering for YAKAP is a two-part process: **selecting** a clinic, and then **completing your first consultation** there to be formally empaneled (officially enrolled as that clinic's patient for the year).

### Step 1: Make Sure You Have an Active PhilHealth PIN

You need a PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN) before you can register with a YAKAP Clinic. If you don't have one yet, you'll need to register as a PhilHealth member first.

### Step 2: Choose a YAKAP Clinic

According to PhilHealth's official YAKAP FAQ, you can select your YAKAP Clinic through:

- The **eGovPH** app
- The **PhilHealth Member Portal** (memberinquiry.philhealth.gov.ph/member/)
- Any **PhilHealth office** nationwide
- **Directly at a YAKAP Clinic**

You'll also need to separately register your qualified dependents, either at the same clinic or a different one that's more convenient for them.

### Step 3: Go for Your First Consultation (First Patient Encounter)

Once you've selected a clinic, visit it in person for your first consultation. PhilHealth's FAQ describes this as where the doctor assesses the patient's condition and, based on that assessment, may recommend needed laboratory tests, prescribe medicine, or refer the patient for cancer screening if there's an indication for it.

### Step 4: Complete Empanelment

Formal enrollment with your chosen clinic — called empanelment — is what establishes your ongoing care relationship with that YAKAP Clinic for the year, per PhilHealth Circular No. 2025-0017. Once empaneled, that clinic becomes the one you return to for future consultations, lab tests, referrals, and medicine prescriptions.

### Step 5: Return for Follow-Up Consultations as Needed

Your doctor determines whether and when you need to come back — for example, for monitoring, follow-up on lab results, or renewal of a prescription. Only the doctor at your registered YAKAP Clinic can issue prescriptions and referrals tied to your YAKAP record.

## What Happens During the Consultation Itself

Based on PhilHealth's official FAQ, a YAKAP consultation generally involves:

- An assessment of your current health condition and relevant history
- A decision on whether laboratory tests are needed — these can be done at the YAKAP Clinic itself, or the clinic may direct you to a partner diagnostic or laboratory facility, still as part of the covered YAKAP service
- A prescription for medicine if clinically needed, which you can then have filled at an accredited GAMOT pharmacy (subject to the ₱20,000 annual medicine limit per beneficiary)
- A referral for a cancer screening test if you show risk factors matching PhilHealth's screening eligibility criteria for breast, lung, liver, or colorectal cancer

PhilHealth's FAQ is explicit that only the doctor at the YAKAP Clinic where you're registered can issue the prescriptions and recommendations needed to access these downstream benefits — a consultation elsewhere generally won't unlock the same YAKAP-linked services.

## Costs: What's Actually Free

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Consultation at an accredited YAKAP Clinic | Free |
| Registration and empanelment | Free |
| Annual clinic fee at a **private** YAKAP Clinic | Up to ₱900 per patient per year |
| Laboratory tests recommended by the doctor | Covered under YAKAP, subject to clinical need |
| Medicines prescribed at the consultation | Free up to ₱20,000 per beneficiary per year at accredited GAMOT pharmacies |

The ₱900 figure, per PhilHealth's official FAQ, applies specifically to registration with a **private** YAKAP Clinic — public/government-run accredited YAKAP Clinics provide these benefits and services free of charge. This fee is separate from, and should not be confused with, charges for the consultation itself, which PhilHealth's FAQ frames as a free benefit once you're registered.

## Common Problems

- **"I selected a clinic online, but I still can't get a free consultation."** Selection alone isn't enough — you must complete your in-person First Patient Encounter and be empaneled before the clinic is obligated to provide ongoing free consultations, per PhilHealth Circular No. 2025-0017.
- **"My chosen clinic is asking for a fee before my consultation."** If it's a private YAKAP Clinic, a fee of up to ₱900 per year is consistent with PhilHealth's FAQ. If a fee is charged at a public/government YAKAP Clinic, or an amount beyond what's described, raise the concern with PhilHealth directly.
- **"I want to switch clinics."** PhilHealth's issuances describe clinic selection and empanelment as tied to a defined process; if you need to change your registered clinic, the safest step is to ask your current YAKAP Clinic or your Local Health Insurance Office how to do this, since the specific re-selection procedure wasn't fully detailed in the sources reviewed for this article.
- **"My consultation didn't result in a referral I expected."** Referrals for labs, medicines, or cancer screening are based on the doctor's clinical assessment against PhilHealth's defined criteria — not on member request. If you disagree with an assessment, you can raise your concerns directly with the clinic or seek a second opinion through the normal healthcare process.

## What to Do If Something Goes Wrong

If you run into a problem with registration, empanelment, or your consultation — such as being denied service at your registered clinic or being charged an unexplained fee — PhilHealth's official YAKAP FAQ lists these contact channels:

- Call the PhilHealth hotline listed on the official YAKAP FAQ page (open 24/7)
- Text or call the Smart/Globe numbers published on PhilHealth's official YAKAP FAQ
- Email actioncenter@philhealth.gov.ph
- Visit any PhilHealth office
- Use the click-to-call feature on the official PhilHealth website

Always verify current contact numbers directly on philhealth.gov.ph, since these can be updated.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is YAKAP the same as PhilHealth Konsulta?**
No. YAKAP replaced and expanded Konsulta, adding a broader medicine benefit and cancer screening coverage on top of the primary care consultations Konsulta already offered.

**Do I need an appointment for a YAKAP consultation?**
PhilHealth's national YAKAP materials reviewed for this article don't specify a uniform appointment system — this can vary by clinic. Check directly with your chosen YAKAP Clinic on how they schedule visits.

**Can I use a different clinic if my registered YAKAP Clinic is closed or inconvenient that day?**
PhilHealth's FAQ and Circular No. 2025-0017 describe ongoing YAKAP care as tied to your empaneled clinic; the sources reviewed don't detail a specific process for one-off visits to a different clinic, so this is best confirmed with PhilHealth or your registered clinic directly.

**What if I don't have a PhilHealth PIN yet?**
You'll need to secure one first — through the PhilHealth Member Portal or any PhilHealth office — before you can register with a YAKAP Clinic.

**Can my whole family register at the same YAKAP Clinic?**
Yes. Members register themselves, and separately register their qualified dependents — either at the same clinic or a different one that's more convenient for each dependent.

**What is the YAKAP Empanelment Slip?**
It's the document that formalizes your enrollment with your chosen YAKAP Clinic, established under PhilHealth Circular No. 2025-0017 as part of completing empanelment.
