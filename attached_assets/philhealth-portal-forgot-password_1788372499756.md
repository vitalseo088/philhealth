## PhilHealth Portal Forgot Password? Here's the Official Way to Reset It (2026)

If you've forgotten your PhilHealth Member Portal password, you can request a reset link directly from PhilHealth's official portal using your PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN) and the email address registered to your account. This guide walks through that process, what to do if your registered email is outdated, and how to avoid fake "PhilHealth" sites while you're trying to recover access.

This guide is for individual members trying to reset access to the **PhilHealth Member Portal** at memberinquiry.philhealth.gov.ph. It does not cover the separate Electronic Premium Reporting System (EPRS) used by employers, or portals used by accredited health facilities, which have their own credentials and support channels.

## Quick Answer

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| You know your PIN and still have access to your registered email | Use the portal's **"Forgot Password?"** link, enter your PIN and email, complete the CAPTCHA, and follow the reset link sent to your email |
| You know your PIN but no longer have access to that email | The reset link cannot reach you — you'll need to have your contact details corrected on your PhilHealth record before you can reset your password (see below) |
| You don't know or have lost your PIN | You'll need to recover your PIN first (through your ID card, MDR, payslip, or PhilHealth directly) before you can use the password reset form |
| A "PhilHealth" site is asking for your password over chat, email, or SMS | Do not respond — PhilHealth resets are done only through the official portal, not through messages asking you to send your password |

## What Is the "Forgot Password" Feature?

The PhilHealth Member Portal's **Forgot Password** page lets a registered member request a password reset link. According to the portal itself, you enter your **PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN)** and the **email address on file** with PhilHealth, complete a CAPTCHA verification, and PhilHealth sends a link to that email so you can set a new password.

This feature only works if:

- You already have a Member Portal account (registration uses your PIN), and
- The email address you enter matches what PhilHealth currently has on record for you.

If either of those doesn't match, the reset will not go through, since the system relies on your PIN and your registered email together to verify your identity.

## Step-by-Step: How to Reset Your PhilHealth Portal Password

1. **Go to the official Member Portal.** Access it through www.philhealth.gov.ph under "Online Services," or directly at memberinquiry.philhealth.gov.ph. Confirm the address bar shows a `philhealth.gov.ph` domain before entering anything.
2. **Click "Forgot Password?"** on the login page.
3. **Enter your PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN)** — your 12-digit member number.
4. **Enter the email address registered to your PhilHealth account.**
5. **Complete the CAPTCHA** shown on the page.
6. **Submit the request.** If your PIN and email match PhilHealth's records, a password reset link will be sent to that email.
7. **Open the email and follow the link** to set a new password.

## If You No Longer Have Access to Your Registered Email

The available official sources do not describe a way to reset your password directly if you can no longer receive email at the address on file — the reset mechanism depends on that email. In that case, your registered contact details need to be corrected first.

Based on PhilHealth's published Anti-Red Tape Act (ARTA) Citizen's Charter for its Member Management Group, updating or correcting information on your Member Data Record — which would include your registered email — can generally be done in one of these ways:

- **Through a PhilHealth Regional Office (PRO) or Local Health Insurance Office (LHIO), in person,** by submitting a duly accomplished PhilHealth Member Registration Form (PMRF) marked for updating, together with a valid photo-bearing ID.
- **By email,** submitting a scanned, duly accomplished PMRF along with a scanned valid ID and a selfie holding that ID, to PhilHealth's Member Management Group at **ofp@philhealth.gov.ph**.

PhilHealth's published service standard for this type of amendment lists a processing time of about 10 minutes for walk-in requests, or around 3 working days for requests submitted by email (with email volume affecting how quickly a specific request is processed). Requirements can vary depending on what exactly is being corrected, so it's worth confirming with PhilHealth or your LHIO what they need for an email-only update versus other types of changes (such as a name or civil status update, which involve additional supporting documents).

Once your email is corrected on PhilHealth's records, you can return to the Member Portal's "Forgot Password?" page and try again.

## If You've Lost or Forgotten Your PIN

The password reset form requires your PIN, so you'll need that first. The available official sources describe the following official ways for members to retrieve their PIN or have PhilHealth verify it:

- Checking your physical PhilHealth ID card, where your PIN is printed, or a payslip if you're an employed member.
- Requesting PIN verification from PhilHealth's Member Management Group, either at a PhilHealth office (walk-in, with a valid ID) or by email to **ofp@philhealth.gov.ph** with a scanned valid ID, a selfie holding that ID, and your full name, date of birth, place of birth, and address.

## Common Problems and What They Usually Mean

- **You submit the form but no email arrives.** Double-check that the email you entered exactly matches what PhilHealth has on file — a mismatch will simply fail to trigger the reset, generally without a specific error message explaining why. Also check your spam or junk folder.
- **You get an error entering your PIN.** Recheck for typos, extra spaces, or transposed digits in your 12-digit PIN.
- **The reset link doesn't work or has expired.** The available official sources don't specify how long a reset link stays valid; if it doesn't work, return to the "Forgot Password?" page and request a new one.
- **You're unsure if the site you're on is the real Member Portal.** Confirm the domain ends in `philhealth.gov.ph` before entering your PIN or any personal information. PhilHealth has formally warned the public about unauthorized websites imitating its portal.

## A Note on Phishing and Fake PhilHealth Sites

PhilHealth has issued public warnings about fake and unauthorized websites that copy the look of its official portal to collect personal or financial information. Under PhilHealth Advisory No. 2026-0024, the Corporation confirmed that certain domains — including one closely resembling "PhilHealth portal" in its name — are **not affiliated with PhilHealth**, and that transactions made through them are not recognized.

Keep these points in mind specifically around password recovery:

- PhilHealth's genuine reset process happens **only** on the official Member Portal — never by replying to an email or text with your password, PIN, or a one-time code.
- Be cautious of any message urging you to "verify your account" or "reactivate your password" through a link sent by SMS from an unidentified number.
- Never enter your PIN, password, or banking details on a site that doesn't end in `.gov.ph`.
- If you suspect you've encountered a fake PhilHealth site or a phishing attempt, report it to PhilHealth's Action Center rather than interacting with it further.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Where do I go to reset my PhilHealth Member Portal password?**
On the official Member Portal login page at memberinquiry.philhealth.gov.ph, click "Forgot Password?" and follow the prompts using your PIN and registered email.

**What information do I need to reset my password?**
Your 12-digit PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN) and the email address currently registered to your PhilHealth account, plus completion of a CAPTCHA check.

**What if the email I use no longer works?**
The reset link is sent only to your registered email, so if you no longer have access to it, you'll need to have PhilHealth correct your contact details first — either by submitting a PMRF at a PRO/LHIO in person, or by email to ofp@philhealth.gov.ph with the required scanned documents.

**Can I reset my password over the phone or by messaging PhilHealth on social media?**
The available official sources describe the reset as something done through the Member Portal itself, not by phone or social media message. Be wary of anyone offering to reset your password through those channels, especially if they ask for your PIN or password directly.

**Is it normal not to receive a reset email right away?**
The official sources reviewed don't specify a guaranteed delivery time. Check your spam/junk folder, confirm the email matches your PhilHealth record exactly, and try again if needed.

**How do I know I'm on the real PhilHealth Member Portal and not a fake one?**
Check that the web address ends in `philhealth.gov.ph`. PhilHealth has publicly identified specific unauthorized look-alike websites and advises members to avoid them.
