# PhilHealth YAKAP Laboratory Tests: What's Covered in 2026

PhilHealth's YAKAP program covers a defined set of outpatient laboratory tests — such as complete blood count, lipid profile, and fasting blood sugar — for members and dependents who are empaneled with a YAKAP Clinic, but only when a YAKAP Clinic doctor determines they're needed. This guide explains what's covered, how you actually get these tests for free, and what to check before assuming a specific test is included.

This is written for PhilHealth members who are already registered — or planning to register — with a YAKAP Clinic and want to understand the laboratory (LABORATORYO) component of the benefit for 2026. One important limitation: PhilHealth's own published FAQ points readers to a detailed annex for the full official list of covered tests rather than listing every test in the FAQ text itself, so this article cross-checks that FAQ against PhilHealth's own public statements to lay out the tests as clearly and accurately as possible.

## What Is the YAKAP Laboratory Benefit?

YAKAP (Yaman ng Kalusugan Program) is PhilHealth's primary care benefit package, built around four components often summarized as **KONSULTA** (consultations), **LABORATORYO** (laboratory tests), **SCREEN** (cancer screening), and **GAMOT** (medicines). The laboratory component — LABORATORYO — is the set of basic diagnostic tests your YAKAP Clinic doctor can order for you, at no cost, as part of your primary care.

According to PhilHealth's official YAKAP FAQ, laboratory tests are listed among the core benefits available "mula sa YAKAP Clinic" (from the YAKAP Clinic), based on the patient's need and the doctor's recommendation — the FAQ directs readers to a detailed annex for the specific list rather than spelling out every test in the FAQ body.

PhilHealth's regional office (PhilHealth-10) publicly described the laboratory tests covered under YAKAP, as reported by the Philippine Information Agency, a Philippine government information office. Based on that statement, and corroborated by later 2026 reporting, the laboratory tests associated with the YAKAP benefit include:

- Complete Blood Count (CBC) with Platelet Count
- Lipid Profile
- Fasting Blood Sugar
- Oral Glucose Tolerance Test
- HbA1c (Hemoglobin A1c)
- Creatinine
- Chest X-ray
- Sputum Microscopy
- Electrocardiogram (ECG)
- Urinalysis
- Pap Smear
- Fecalysis
- Fecal Occult Blood Test

**Important caveat:** PhilHealth's own FAQ does not print this list directly in the document reviewed for this article — it refers readers to an annex published separately. The list above reflects how PhilHealth's regional office and subsequent 2026 news coverage have publicly described the benefit. If you need the exact, currently effective annex for legal or clinical purposes, ask your YAKAP Clinic or a PhilHealth Local Health Insurance Office (LHIO) to confirm the current official list, since annexes to PhilHealth issuances can be revised.

## Who Is Eligible for the YAKAP Laboratory Benefit?

Per PhilHealth's official FAQ, this benefit is available to **PhilHealth members and their qualified dependents** who are registered and empaneled with a YAKAP Clinic. You don't request specific lab tests on your own — eligibility for any individual test depends on your registered YAKAP Clinic doctor's clinical assessment during your consultation.

## How Laboratory Tests Are Covered Under YAKAP

### The Tests Are Doctor-Directed, Not Member-Selected

PhilHealth's FAQ is explicit that the laboratory tests, cancer screening tests, and medicines under YAKAP are made available "batay sa kailangan ng pasyente at rekomendasyon ng doktor" — based on the patient's need and the doctor's recommendation. This means you cannot simply walk in and request any test from the covered list; your YAKAP Clinic doctor decides which tests are clinically appropriate for you based on your consultation.

### Only Your Registered Clinic's Doctor Can Order Them

The FAQ also states that only the doctor at the YAKAP Clinic where you are registered can issue the referral or recommendation needed to access laboratory tests, screening tests, and medicines under the program.

### Where the Tests Actually Happen

If your YAKAP Clinic has its own laboratory facilities, the tests may be done on-site. If it doesn't, PhilHealth's FAQ notes that the clinic will direct you to a partner diagnostic or laboratory facility to have the recommended tests done — this remains part of the YAKAP service and is not billed to you separately for the covered items.

## Step-by-Step: Getting a Covered Lab Test Through YAKAP

1. **Be registered and empaneled with a YAKAP Clinic.** You need to have selected a clinic and completed your First Patient Encounter (FPE) there.
2. **Attend a consultation.** Visit your registered clinic when you have a health concern, or for your scheduled check-up or follow-up.
3. **Let the doctor assess your condition.** Based on your history and examination, the doctor determines whether any of the covered laboratory tests are needed.
4. **Receive the doctor's recommendation or referral.** If a test is needed and your clinic doesn't perform it on-site, the doctor's office will direct you to the appropriate partner facility.
5. **Have the test done.** Present yourself at the clinic's own laboratory or the referred partner facility as instructed.
6. **Return for your results and follow-up.** Per the FAQ, your doctor may ask you to return for a follow-up consultation depending on your results and ongoing monitoring needs.

## Laboratory Tests vs. Cancer Screening Tests: Know the Difference

PhilHealth's FAQ treats general laboratory tests and cancer screening tests as **separate components** of YAKAP, each governed by its own annex. The officially listed cancer screening tests, according to PhilHealth's FAQ, are:

- Mammogram
- Breast ultrasound
- Low-dose chest CT scan
- Alpha fetoprotein
- Liver ultrasound
- Colonoscopy

Like the general laboratory tests, these screening tests require a referral from your YAKAP Clinic doctor and are only available to beneficiaries whose clinical situation indicates a need for them — you cannot go directly to a screening facility without that referral. The FAQ confirms that once a referral is issued, the screening facility should not charge the patient for the covered test.

## Costs and Coverage

| Item | Cost to Member (Per Official Sources) |
|---|---|
| Covered lab tests at a **public** YAKAP Clinic | Free — PhilHealth Advisory No. 2026-0003 states covered consultations, basic laboratory services, and other included primary care services at public facilities must be provided free of charge |
| Covered lab tests at a **private** YAKAP Clinic | Included within the private clinic's overall co-payment cap of ₱900 per member, per year, under the same advisory |
| Covered cancer screening tests, with a valid referral | No additional charge to the patient, per PhilHealth's YAKAP FAQ |
| Tests not covered by the annex, or not recommended by your doctor | Not part of the free YAKAP benefit; PhilHealth's materials reviewed do not describe coverage for self-requested tests outside a doctor's recommendation |

PhilHealth coverage for any specific laboratory or screening test ultimately depends on the applicable benefit annex, your doctor's clinical recommendation, and your eligibility as an empaneled beneficiary — this is a summary of the general rule, not a guarantee that a particular test will be approved in a particular case.

## Common Problems

- **Assuming any lab test can be requested on demand.** Coverage is tied to your doctor's clinical recommendation, not member choice — a test you want but that isn't clinically indicated may not be covered.
- **Going to a lab test facility without a referral from your registered clinic.** PhilHealth's FAQ is clear that only your registered YAKAP Clinic's doctor can issue the referral needed to access these free tests.
- **Being asked to pay for a covered test at a public YAKAP Clinic.** This should not happen for included services — see the co-payment rules above.
- **Confusion between the general laboratory list and the cancer screening list.** These are separate components with separate annexes and separate referral triggers.

## What to Do If Something Goes Wrong

If you believe you were incorrectly charged for a covered laboratory test at a public YAKAP Clinic, or denied a test your doctor recommended, PhilHealth Advisory No. 2026-0003 directs members to raise the concern with PhilHealth's Corporate Action Center. Confirm current contact channels directly on philhealth.gov.ph, since contact details can be updated over time.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Are all laboratory tests free under PhilHealth YAKAP?**
No. Only tests within the applicable benefit annex, and recommended by your registered YAKAP Clinic doctor based on clinical need, are covered as part of the free benefit.

**Can I ask my YAKAP Clinic for a specific lab test I want?**
You can raise your health concerns with your doctor, but per PhilHealth's official FAQ, which tests are ordered depends on the doctor's clinical assessment — not a direct member request.

**Do I need to pay if my clinic sends me to a different laboratory?**
If the test is part of your YAKAP-covered benefit and your clinic refers you to a partner facility, PhilHealth's FAQ describes this as part of the covered service, not a separate paid transaction — though you should confirm this at the point of referral, since practices can vary by locality.

**Is a chest X-ray covered under YAKAP?**
Publicly reported descriptions of the YAKAP laboratory benefit, including from PhilHealth's own regional office, include chest X-ray among the covered tests — subject to your doctor's recommendation and the currently effective annex.

**What's the difference between YAKAP's lab tests and cancer screening tests?**
They are two separate benefit components under YAKAP, each with its own list and its own referral requirement from your YAKAP Clinic doctor.
