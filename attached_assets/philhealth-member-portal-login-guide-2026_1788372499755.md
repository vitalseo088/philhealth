## What Is the PhilHealth Member Portal?

The member portal is PhilHealth's online system for individual members to manage parts of their PhilHealth membership without going to a branch in person. This can include things like:

- Viewing your Member Data Record (MDR)
- Checking your contribution and payment history
- Updating certain personal details
- Downloading or printing membership documents online
- Other self-service features currently offered **[VERIFY: the complete, current list of self-service features — this has changed over time as PhilHealth has moved services between systems]**

Because PhilHealth has consolidated or replaced online systems in the past, confirm on the official site which platform is currently used for member login before assuming this guide's steps match exactly.

## Who Can Access the Member Portal?

In general, any registered PhilHealth member with a valid PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN) can set up member portal access, including:

- Employed members
- Self-paying (individually paying) members — meaning members who pay their own contributions rather than through an employer
- Voluntary members
- OFWs (Overseas Filipino Workers)
- Other recognized member categories

Dependents generally don't get a separate portal login — their records are typically viewed through the principal member's account. **[VERIFY: current rules, if any, on separate dependent access]**
