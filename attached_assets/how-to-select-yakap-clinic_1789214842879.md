# How to Select a YAKAP Clinic: A 2026 PhilHealth Guide

If you're a PhilHealth member, you select a YAKAP clinic by choosing one PhilHealth-accredited primary care provider — through the eGovPH app, the PhilHealth Member Portal, a PhilHealth Local Health Insurance Office (LHIO), or by walking into the clinic itself — and then completing your First Patient Encounter there to be officially empaneled. This guide walks through that process for 2026, what to check before you pick a clinic, and what the selection actually commits you to.

This is written for PhilHealth members and dependents who are choosing a YAKAP clinic for the first time, or who want to understand the process before registering. Some details below — such as the exact steps for transferring to a different clinic after you've already been empaneled — are not fully spelled out in the official PhilHealth materials reviewed for this article, and that is noted where relevant rather than guessed at.

## What Is a YAKAP Clinic?

YAKAP stands for **Yaman ng Kalusugan Program**, PhilHealth's primary care benefit package. A YAKAP Clinic is a health facility — this can be a government Rural Health Unit, a city health center, a private clinic, or an outpatient department of a hospital — that has been accredited by PhilHealth specifically to deliver this primary care package to members who register, or "empanel," with them.

Once you select and empanel with a YAKAP Clinic, that facility becomes your main point of contact for:

- Free check-ups and consultations
- Basic laboratory tests, based on your doctor's assessment
- Referrals for cancer screening tests when medically indicated
- Prescriptions for medicines available through PhilHealth's GAMOT program, up to a benefit limit per beneficiary per year

According to PhilHealth's own YAKAP FAQ, only your registered YAKAP Clinic's doctor can issue the referrals and prescriptions needed to access these laboratory, screening, and medicine benefits — so which clinic you select matters for how you actually use the program.

A separate PhilHealth advisory (Advisory No. 2026-0023) clarifies an important eligibility rule for the clinics themselves: accreditation as a YAKAP Provider is limited to primary care providers operating from **fixed and permanent physical clinics**. Mobile health units and telemedicine-only setups are not considered valid, stand-alone YAKAP Clinics and cannot be the basis for empanelment — a distinction PhilHealth issued specifically to address outreach events being misrepresented as YAKAP registration.

## Who Is Eligible to Select a YAKAP Clinic?

Per PhilHealth's official YAKAP FAQ, the program is open to **every Filipino** who is a PhilHealth member, along with their qualified dependents. To select a clinic, you need an active PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN). If you don't have one yet, you can secure it through the PhilHealth Member Portal or any PhilHealth office before proceeding with YAKAP registration.

Each qualified dependent can be registered at the same YAKAP Clinic as the principal member, or at a different YAKAP Clinic — for example, if a dependent lives elsewhere and a different clinic is more convenient for them.

## How to Select a YAKAP Clinic: Step-by-Step

Based on PhilHealth's official YAKAP FAQ, there are several official channels for selecting and registering with a YAKAP Clinic.

### Step 1: Make Sure You Have an Active PhilHealth PIN

You'll need your PhilHealth Identification Number before you can register. If you're unsure of your PIN or membership status, you can check it through the PhilHealth Member Portal or by visiting a PhilHealth office.

### Step 2: Choose Your Channel to Select a Clinic

PhilHealth's official FAQ lists these channels for selecting a YAKAP Clinic and registering:

- **eGovPH app** — view the list of accredited YAKAP Clinics and select one
- **PhilHealth Member Portal** (memberinquiry.philhealth.gov.ph) — log in and select from the available list of accredited providers
- **PhilHealth offices nationwide** — walk in and ask staff to assist with clinic selection
- **The YAKAP Clinic itself** — some members select and register directly at the clinic they intend to use

### Step 3: Review the List of Accredited Clinics Before Choosing

Only clinics that appear on PhilHealth's official accredited YAKAP list are valid choices. PhilHealth publishes and periodically updates this list on its official website. Since only accredited, fixed-location clinics qualify, it's worth confirming a clinic's accreditation status through the eGovPH app, the Member Portal, or by calling PhilHealth directly, rather than relying only on a clinic's own signage or a third-party website.

### Step 4: Complete Your First Patient Encounter (FPE)

Selecting a clinic is not the same as being fully registered. Once you've made your choice, PhilHealth's FAQ instructs members to go to that clinic for their **first consultation, or First Patient Encounter**. This is when the clinic's doctor conducts your initial assessment.

### Step 5: Understand That Empanelment Creates an Ongoing Relationship

Per PhilHealth Advisory No. 2026-0023, empanelment under YAKAP is meant to establish **continuity of care** — an ongoing clinical relationship with your chosen provider — rather than a one-time visit. The advisory states that a member can only be validly empaneled to a fixed and permanent YAKAP Clinic of their choice.

The official materials reviewed for this article confirm that this ongoing relationship exists and that only your registered clinic's doctor can issue prescriptions and referrals for you under the program. However, they do not spell out, in the sources checked, a specific minimum enrollment period or a detailed step-by-step procedure for switching to a different clinic after empanelment. If you need to transfer, the safest approach is to ask directly at a PhilHealth LHIO or through the PhilHealth Member Portal, since this is the kind of procedural detail that can change and should be confirmed at the time you need it.

## What to Check Before You Pick a Clinic

PhilHealth's own materials don't rank clinics or tell you which one to choose — that decision is yours. Based on what the official FAQ and advisories describe about how the program works, these are practical things worth confirming beforehand:

- **Accreditation status.** Confirm the clinic is on PhilHealth's current accredited YAKAP list, since accreditation periods can expire and lists are updated periodically.
- **Facility type.** Make sure it's a fixed, permanent clinic — not a mobile unit or telemedicine-only service, which PhilHealth advisory rules exclude from YAKAP accreditation.
- **Public vs. private.** Under PhilHealth Advisory No. 2026-0003, YAKAP services at **public** clinics must be provided free of charge. At **private** YAKAP clinics, your co-payment is capped at ₱900 per member, per year — after which remaining covered services for the year should be provided at no additional cost.
- **Whether it's practical for follow-ups.** Since your registered clinic's doctor is the only one who can issue your GAMOT prescriptions and screening referrals, consider how easy it will be for you to return there for consultations and monitoring.
- **Separate clinics for dependents, if useful.** If a dependent lives in a different area, they can be registered at a different YAKAP Clinic than yours.

## Your Rights as a YAKAP Member

Per PhilHealth Advisory No. 2026-0003, once you've selected and been empaneled with a clinic, you have specific rights:

1. **No fees at public YAKAP clinics.** Covered consultations, basic laboratory services, and other included primary care services at public facilities must be provided free of charge.
2. **Capped co-payment at private clinics.** Your out-of-pocket co-payment at a private YAKAP clinic is limited to ₱900 per member per year. Once that cap is reached, remaining covered YAKAP services for the year should be provided without additional charge.
3. **Continuous care.** Once you've completed your First Patient Encounter and signed your YAKAP Empanelment Slip, you have the right to continuous and appropriate primary care from your empaneled clinic.

If you're asked to pay fees that should be covered, or denied services despite being properly empaneled, PhilHealth's advisory directs members to raise the concern with its Corporate Action Center (24/7 hotline, SMS, or email — see the Official Sources section below for the current channels listed in that advisory).

## GAMOT Benefit: What Your Chosen Clinic Unlocks

Selecting a YAKAP Clinic is also what gives you access to PhilHealth's GAMOT medicine benefit. According to the official FAQ:

- Only a doctor at your registered YAKAP Clinic can prescribe medicines under this benefit.
- Prescriptions are issued through the GAMOT App, which accredited pharmacies use to validate and dispense the medicines.
- Each beneficiary — the principal member and each qualified dependent separately — has an annual benefit limit of up to ₱20,000 worth of medicines.
- Maintenance medicines may be prescribed by the clinic doctor for up to three months at a time, but pharmacies typically dispense a one-month supply per visit, with the prescription remaining valid for members to return for refills.
- A separate PhilHealth advisory (cited in multiple PhilHealth-related reports as Advisory No. 2026-0007) states that a defined list of essential medicines is intended to remain continuously available at YAKAP clinics; PhilHealth's official YAKAP FAQ additionally notes that a broader GAMOT medicine list is published separately by the agency.

## Examples

He registers himself at a private YAKAP-accredited clinic close to his office, but registers his children at a different YAKAP Clinic nearer their school, since PhilHealth allows dependents to be empaneled at a clinic different from the principal member's.

## Common Problems

- **A clinic isn't accepting new registrations.** Some facilities may reach capacity. If this happens, PhilHealth's official channels — the eGovPH app, Member Portal, or an LHIO — can help you find another accredited option nearby.
- **Confusing a health caravan or outreach event with YAKAP registration.** PhilHealth Advisory No. 2026-0023 specifically warns that mobile health units and telemedicine providers cannot serve as the basis for valid YAKAP empanelment, even if they conduct community outreach. Only registration with a fixed, permanent, accredited clinic counts.
- **Being charged at a public clinic.** This should not happen for covered YAKAP services — see "Your Rights as a YAKAP Member" above.
- **Uncertainty about which clinic a member or dependent is currently registered to.** PhilHealth's FAQ and related advisories note that members should be able to verify their registered provider through their Member Information/Member Portal record, or by asking a PhilHealth office directly.

## What to Do If Something Goes Wrong

If you experience a problem with YAKAP clinic selection, registration, or service delivery, the official advisory reviewed for this article directs members to PhilHealth's Corporate Action Center rather than the clinic alone. Contact details change from time to time, so confirm current numbers and emails directly on philhealth.gov.ph before reaching out, or use the contact information published in the specific advisory linked in the Official Sources section below.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is selecting a YAKAP clinic mandatory?**
PhilHealth's YAKAP FAQ frames registration with a YAKAP Clinic as necessary in order to access the program's free benefits and services — you need to register with a chosen clinic to use YAKAP.

**Can I select a YAKAP clinic that is far from where I live?**
The official sources reviewed for this article do not specify a fixed maximum distance for clinic selection. As a practical matter, since you'll need to return to your chosen clinic for consultations, laboratory referrals, and prescriptions, choosing one that's realistically reachable for follow-up visits is worth factoring into your decision.

**Do I need to pay to register at a YAKAP clinic?**
Official PhilHealth materials describe co-payment rules for services received (free at public clinics; capped at ₱900/year at private clinics), but do not describe a separate registration fee.

**Can I select a different YAKAP clinic for my dependents?**
Yes. PhilHealth's official FAQ confirms dependents may be registered at the same clinic as the principal member or at a different YAKAP Clinic.

**What happens after I select a clinic?**
You then need to visit that clinic for your First Patient Encounter, where the clinic's doctor conducts your initial consultation and assessment. This, together with signing the YAKAP Empanelment Slip, is what completes your empanelment.

**Can a mobile clinic or telemedicine provider be my YAKAP clinic?**
No. Per PhilHealth Advisory No. 2026-0023, only fixed and permanent physical clinics qualify for YAKAP accreditation; mobile health units and telemedicine-only providers do not.
