# PhilHealth Requirements to Change Name: Documents and Steps (2026)

To change or correct your name in PhilHealth, you generally file a **request to update your member record** using PhilHealth's member registration form (PMRF) and submit a **supporting civil registry document** that proves the new or correct name, such as a PSA-issued marriage certificate or birth certificate. The exact documents depend on *why* your name is changing. This guide explains the PhilHealth requirements to change name in plain language so you can prepare before visiting PhilHealth or going to your HR.

This guide is for employed, self-paying, and other PhilHealth members. It is written for 2026, but forms and procedures can change, so confirm the latest steps with PhilHealth before you file.

## What Does "Change Name" Mean in PhilHealth?

Your **Member Data Record (MDR)** is the official record PhilHealth keeps about you, including your name, birthdate, civil status, and dependents. Requests involving your name usually fall into one of two groups:

- **Change of name:** your legal name has changed, for example after marriage, or after a court-recognized change.
- **Correction of entry:** your record has a mistake, such as a misspelled name or wrong middle name, and needs to match your birth certificate.

PhilHealth may treat these differently, and the supporting documents are not necessarily the same. Ask which category your request falls under before you submit.

## Who Needs to Update Their Name?

You may need to update your PhilHealth record if:

- You changed your surname after getting married
- You changed back to your maiden name after the recognition of an annulment, nullity, or other legal event
- Your name was misspelled when you registered
- Your name on PhilHealth does not match your birth certificate or IDs
- A court or civil registry has ordered a change in your name

### Documents That Are Commonly Useful in Every Case

- Your PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN) or a copy of your current MDR
- At least one valid government-issued ID
- Photocopies of your supporting documents, in addition to the originals

### Why Your Documents Must Match

Updates are often delayed because of mismatches. Check that these agree across your form, supporting documents, and IDs:

- Spelling of first, middle, and last name
- Suffixes (Jr., III)
- Birthdate
- Civil status

If your IDs still show your old name, expect questions, and ask PhilHealth what they accept.

## How to Change Your Name in PhilHealth

The steps below describe a general process. Your exact steps may differ by membership category.

1. **Identify your situation.** Decide whether you are changing your name (for example, after marriage) or correcting an error.
2. **Get the current PMRF** from PhilHealth and indicate that you are updating your information. **[VERIFY form version, download options, and the section used for updates]**
3. **Fill in your details** with your correct information and your PIN.
4. **Prepare supporting documents.** Use PSA-issued copies when possible, plus a valid ID.
5. **Submit your request.**
   - *Employed members:* usually through your employer's HR or PhilHealth officer.
   - *Self-paying and other members:* at a PhilHealth office or Local Health Insurance Office.
   - Ask whether the Member Portal allows name updates. This guide does not confirm that. **[VERIFY]**
6. **Confirm the update.** After processing, check that your MDR shows the correct name. Keep copies of everything you submitted.

## After You Update Your Name

- **Tell your employer.** If you are an employed member, your employer's records should match your PhilHealth record so contributions are posted correctly.
- **Check your dependents.** If your name changed after marriage, ask PhilHealth how to declare your spouse as a dependent. The spouse rules are covered in a separate guide.
- **Check your contributions record.** Confirm that your past contributions appear under your record.
- **Bring updated documents to hospitals.** Hospitals check records, and a mismatch can cause delays during an admission.

## Common Problems

- **Wrong category of request.** A correction may need different documents than a name change. Ask PhilHealth which applies.
- **Documents not matching.** Differences in spelling between your PMRF and your supporting document are a common cause of delay.
- **No PSA copy yet.** Ask PhilHealth what alternatives, if any, are accepted. **[VERIFY]**
- **Duplicate records.** If you suspect you have more than one PhilHealth record or PIN, ask PhilHealth to review and merge records instead of registering again.
- **Records not updated.** Processing may still be ongoing, or the form may be incomplete.

## What To Do If Something Goes Wrong

1. Keep copies of everything you submitted and any acknowledgment slip.
2. Check your MDR through official PhilHealth channels.
3. Visit the PhilHealth office or Local Health Insurance Office that handles your record and ask for the status.
4. Use the official hotline or email channels listed on the PhilHealth website. Avoid numbers from unofficial pages.
5. If an update is urgent because of a hospital admission, ask the hospital's PhilHealth coordinator for help with verification.
