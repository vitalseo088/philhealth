## PhilHealth Dialysis Sessions Per Year: How Many Are Covered in 2026

Here's the direct answer: PhilHealth currently covers **up to 156 hemodialysis sessions per year** for members and dependents diagnosed with Chronic Kidney Disease Stage 5 (CKD5), at a case rate of **₱6,350 per session** — a total annual package of roughly **₱990,600**. This limit and rate were set by PhilHealth Circular No. 2024-0023 and remain in effect through 2026, with no official PhilHealth issuance found that has since changed either figure.

This guide is for PhilHealth members, dependents, and caregivers managing ongoing dialysis treatment who want to understand the actual session limit, how the case rate works, what's included, and what to do if more sessions are medically needed than the annual allowance covers.

> **Important limitation:** You may see other figures online — 90 sessions, 144 sessions, or rates of ₱2,600 or ₱4,000 per session. These are outdated. PhilHealth expanded the annual limit from 90 to 156 sessions in 2023 (PhilHealth Circular No. 2023-0009) and raised the rate twice in 2024 — first to ₱4,000, then to ₱6,350 (PhilHealth Circular No. 2024-0023), effective October 9, 2024. The current figures for 2026 are 156 sessions at ₱6,350 each.

## What Is the PhilHealth Hemodialysis Benefit?

Hemodialysis is a treatment that mechanically filters waste and excess fluid from the blood when the kidneys can no longer do so — typically needed by patients with **Chronic Kidney Disease Stage 5 (CKD5)**, also called end-stage renal disease (ESRD). PhilHealth's hemodialysis benefit is a **case rate**: a fixed amount paid to the accredited dialysis facility per session, generally deducted directly from the patient's bill rather than paid out as cash.

The 156-session annual allowance reflects the standard clinical schedule of **three hemodialysis sessions per week for 52 weeks** (3 × 52 = 156). This session count was institutionalized through **PhilHealth Circular No. 2023-0009**, which expanded the previous 90-session annual limit, and the current per-session rate of ₱6,350 was set through **PhilHealth Circular No. 2024-0023**, which took effect October 9, 2024 and raised the rate from an earlier ₱4,000 figure set only a few months prior in mid-2024.

## Who Is Eligible?

Based on the governing circulars, coverage under the 156-session hemodialysis package requires:

- A diagnosis of **Chronic Kidney Disease Stage 5 (CKD5)** — defined officially as end-stage renal disease resulting in irreversible loss of nearly all kidney function.
- **Registration in the PhilHealth Dialysis Database (PDD)**, per PhilHealth Circular No. 2016-0007. Facilities generally handle this registration when a patient begins filing hemodialysis claims.
- A hemodialysis prescription/order from the patient's attending physician, specifying the number of sessions needed.
- Treatment at a **PhilHealth-accredited hemodialysis facility** — hospital-based or a stand-alone accredited dialysis center.
- Standard PhilHealth membership eligibility rules, applicable to members and their qualified dependents.

The available official sources reviewed for this guide do not specify a separate contribution-count requirement distinct from general PhilHealth membership rules for this particular benefit. If you're unsure of your own eligibility status, confirm directly through the PhilHealth Member Portal or a PhilHealth office rather than relying on unofficial figures.

## How the 156-Session Limit Works

A few rules govern how the annual session count is applied:

- **The count resets each calendar year** and does not roll over — unused sessions from one year cannot be carried into the next.
- **The 45-day annual confinement limit that applies to general inpatient benefits does not apply to this hemodialysis package.** This is a common point of confusion: PhilHealth's standard hospitalization benefit gives each member/dependent group a shared 45 hospitalization days per year, but the dedicated 156-session hemodialysis benefit is specifically exempted from that limit, per PhilHealth Circular No. 2023-0009.
- **If a hemodialysis session takes place during a hospital confinement**, the dialysis claim is filed separately from the inpatient claim and should not overlap with it.
- The facility administering treatment is responsible for **monitoring and reporting the number of sessions used** per patient per year.

## What the ₱6,350 Case Rate Includes

According to PhilHealth's own description of the benefit expansion, the hemodialysis case rate is meant to cover, per session:

- Use of the dialysis facility and machine
- Philippine National Drug Formulary (PNDF) medicines as needed, including anti-coagulation medications and drugs for anemia management
- IV solutions, dialysis supplies, and the dialyzer
- Administrative costs such as facility fee, utilities, and staff time
- Standard professional fees for the session

**What may cost extra:** patients requiring services beyond this defined minimum standard of care — for example, telemedicine consultation or immediate interventions for complications during a session — may be charged an additional professional fee, **capped at ₱450 per instance** according to PhilHealth's own announcement of the rate increase. Facilities are required to explain any such co-payment to the patient.

PhilHealth coverage depends on the applicable benefit package and eligibility requirements — the case rate is not a guarantee that every possible cost at every facility will be covered in full; it establishes the standard of care PhilHealth pays for and caps what can be charged for that defined standard.

## No Balance Billing for Basic Services

The hemodialysis package is covered under PhilHealth's **No Balance Billing (NBB) Policy** (PhilHealth Circular No. 2017-0017) for the essential services described above. In practice, this means an accredited facility should not charge a patient extra for the standard package of services already covered by the ₱6,350 case rate — additional charges are meant to be limited to genuinely additional or premium services beyond that standard.

## Step-by-Step: How Coverage Is Applied

1. **Get a formal CKD5 diagnosis and a hemodialysis prescription** from your attending physician or nephrologist.
2. **Choose a PhilHealth-accredited dialysis facility** — a hospital-based dialysis unit or an accredited stand-alone dialysis center.
3. **Register in the PhilHealth Dialysis Database (PDD)** — typically arranged by the facility when you begin treatment.
4. **Attend your scheduled sessions.** The facility tracks how many of your 156 annual sessions have been used.
5. **The facility files the claim** and deducts the ₱6,350 case rate from your bill per session.
6. **You pay only the balance, if any** — which should be limited to any additional services beyond the standard package, subject to the ₱450 professional-fee cap where applicable.

## Peritoneal Dialysis: A Separate Coverage Track

Hemodialysis is not the only PhilHealth-covered renal replacement therapy. **Peritoneal dialysis (PD)** — where the lining of the abdomen is used to filter the blood, often performed at home — is covered under a different, non-session-based package. Per PhilHealth's official Z Benefits schedule, the **"PD First" package for end-stage renal disease requiring peritoneal dialysis is currently listed at ₱270,000 per year**, subject to its own separate clinical eligibility criteria (including having a properly placed Tenckhoff catheter and being adequately trained to perform PD at home). This is a distinct benefit track from the 156-session hemodialysis package described above — the two are not interchangeable, and a patient's clinical situation determines which is medically appropriate, a decision for their physician to make.

## Common Problems and What to Do

**You've used all 156 sessions for the year but still need dialysis before the year ends.**
Based on available official sources, unused sessions do not carry over, and there is no indication of an official provision allowing additional PhilHealth-covered sessions beyond 156 within the same calendar year under the current circular. Sessions beyond the limit would need to be covered out-of-pocket or through other assistance mechanisms (for example, a hospital's Malasakit Center, LGU medical assistance, or DOH-administered programs) — ask your facility's PhilHealth or social welfare desk what support may be available locally.

**A facility charged you for something you believe should be included in the case rate.**
This may be a No Balance Billing policy concern. Document the charge and raise it with the facility's PhilHealth officer first; if unresolved, you can escalate to PhilHealth's Corporate Action Center at (02) 8662-2588 or actioncenter@philhealth.gov.ph.

**You're not sure if you're registered in the PhilHealth Dialysis Database.**
Ask your dialysis facility directly, since registration is typically handled through the facility as part of your first hemodialysis claim.

**You've heard about a bill to expand dialysis coverage further.**
As of this writing, a bill titled the "PhilHealth Hemodialysis Expansion Act" has been filed in the House of Representatives proposing further increases to dialysis coverage. This is **proposed legislation, not a current PhilHealth policy** — it would require passage into law and subsequent PhilHealth implementing guidelines before taking effect. Treat any claims of a rate or session increase beyond 156 sessions/₱6,350 as unconfirmed until PhilHealth issues an official circular.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many dialysis sessions does PhilHealth cover per year?**
Up to 156 hemodialysis sessions per year for CKD5 patients, based on the standard schedule of three sessions per week for 52 weeks, under PhilHealth Circular No. 2024-0023.

**How much does PhilHealth pay per dialysis session?**
₱6,350 per hemodialysis session as of the rate that took effect October 9, 2024, which remains the applicable rate through 2026 based on available official sources.

**Do unused sessions carry over to the next year?**
No. Based on official PhilHealth statements about the 156-session package, unused sessions in one year are not carried over to the following year.

**Does the 45-day hospital confinement limit apply to dialysis?**
No — the dedicated 156-session hemodialysis package is specifically exempted from PhilHealth's general 45-day annual inpatient confinement limit.

**Is peritoneal dialysis covered the same way as hemodialysis?**
No. Peritoneal dialysis is covered under a separate annual package (currently listed at ₱270,000/year for the "PD First" package) with its own eligibility criteria, rather than the per-session hemodialysis structure.

**Do I need to register somewhere before starting PhilHealth-covered dialysis?**
Yes — registration in the PhilHealth Dialysis Database (PDD) is required, and this is typically handled by the treating facility.

**Will PhilHealth cover dialysis if I'm confined in the hospital at the same time?**
The hemodialysis session is filed as a separate claim from the inpatient confinement claim and should not overlap with it, based on the governing circular.
