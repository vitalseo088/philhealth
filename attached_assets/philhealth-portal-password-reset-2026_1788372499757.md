# PhilHealth Portal Password Reset: How to Regain Access (2026)

A PhilHealth portal password reset is done through the **Forgot Password** page on the official Member Portal, using your PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN) and the email address linked to your account. PhilHealth sends a reset link to that email, and you use it to set a new password.

This guide is for individual PhilHealth members who already have a Member Portal account but can't log in because they forgot their password. It does not cover recovering a lost PIN, employer (EPRS) password resets, or healthcare-provider (IHCP) account access, which use separate processes.

## Quick Answer

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| You forgot your Member Portal password | Go to the official login page and click **Forgot Password** |
| You need to reset it | Enter your **PIN** and your **registered email address**, then complete the CAPTCHA |
| After submitting | PhilHealth sends a **password reset link to that email** |
| You don't have portal access to your email anymore, or the reset doesn't work | See the "Common Problems" section below |

**Official page:** `https://memberinquiry.philhealth.gov.ph/member/forgotPassword.xhtml`

Reach this page only through the official Member Portal login screen or www.philhealth.gov.ph. PhilHealth has publicly warned members to check that a site's address ends in ".gov.ph" — not ".com" or ".net" — before entering any PIN, password, or personal details, because look-alike sites have been used to steal member credentials.

## What Is the PhilHealth Portal Password Reset Process?

When you create a Member Portal account, you set your own password, separate from your PhilHealth PIN. If you forget that password, you don't need to create a new account or reapply for a PIN — you use the **Forgot Password** facility built into the same portal at `memberinquiry.philhealth.gov.ph`.

Based on the official Forgot Password page, the process works by having you enter your PIN and your registered email address; PhilHealth then emails you a link that lets you set a new password. This is separate from the **Create Account** process (for people who have never registered on the portal) and separate from the **PIN Application** process (for people who don't have a PhilHealth Identification Number at all).

## Who This Applies To

This reset process applies to individual members who:

- Already **created a Member Portal account** using a valid PIN, and
- Still have access to the **email address** they registered with

If you never created a portal account in the first place, "Forgot Password" won't help you — you'll need **Create Account** instead. If you've lost your PIN entirely, that's a separate PIN-recovery situation, not a password reset.

## What You'll Need

Based on the official Forgot Password form, have the following ready:

- Your **12-digit PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN)**
- The **email address** you used when you created your Member Portal account
- Access to that **email inbox**, since the reset link is sent there
- A few minutes to complete a **CAPTCHA**

The official page does not list an alternative recovery method (such as a mobile number or security question) for the password reset step itself — only PIN and email are requested.

## Step-by-Step: Resetting Your PhilHealth Portal Password

1. **Go to the official Member Portal login page.**
   Visit `memberinquiry.philhealth.gov.ph/member/login.xhtml`, or reach it through the Member Portal link on www.philhealth.gov.ph.

2. **Click "Forgot Password?"**
   This link sits on the login page next to "Create Account" and "PhilHealth Member Registration."

3. **Enter your PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN).**
   Use the same 12-digit PIN linked to your account.

4. **Enter your registered email address.**
   This must match the email address on file with your Member Portal account, not just any email you own.

5. **Complete the CAPTCHA and click "Send."**

6. **Check your email inbox.**
   Look for a message from PhilHealth containing a password reset link. If you don't see it within a few minutes, check your spam or junk folder.

7. **Open the reset link and set a new password.**
   Your new password should follow the same rules used when the portal was originally set up: 8–32 characters, with at least one digit, one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, and one special character.

8. **Return to the login page and sign in** using your PIN and new password.

## Common Problems and What to Do

**"I entered my PIN and email, but I never got a reset email."**
First, check spam/junk folders. Confirm that the email address you entered is exactly the one registered on your Member Portal account — a reset email won't arrive if the address doesn't match what PhilHealth has on file, even if it's an email you actually own. If you're certain the details are correct and still receive nothing after a reasonable wait, it's best to contact PhilHealth directly rather than repeatedly resubmitting the form.

**"I don't have access to my registered email anymore."**
The official Forgot Password page only asks for a PIN and email — it does not describe an alternative self-service path if you've lost access to that email. In this situation, contacting PhilHealth directly to verify your identity and update your records is the appropriate next step, rather than attempting to create a second account.

**"My reset link says it's expired or invalid."**
The official page doesn't publish how long a reset link stays valid. If a link doesn't work, the safest option is to return to the Forgot Password page and request a new one rather than trying the old link repeatedly.

**"I reset my password, but I still can't log in."**
Double-check that Caps Lock is off, that you're entering the new password exactly as you set it, and that you're on the correct official URL. If problems continue, use Forgot Password again to set a fresh password rather than guessing repeatedly.

**"I'm being asked to pay someone to 'unlock' or 'reset' my PhilHealth account."**
This is not a legitimate part of the official process. PhilHealth's Forgot Password page requires only your PIN, your email, and a CAPTCHA — nothing more, and no fee.

## What to Do If Something Goes Wrong

- **Never enter your PIN or password on a non-".gov.ph" site.** PhilHealth has specifically cautioned members to verify the domain and the padlock/HTTPS indicator before logging in, following past reports of phishing attempts targeting member accounts.
- **Don't repeatedly resubmit the Forgot Password form** if you're not receiving the email — this is unlikely to fix a mismatched-email issue and may be flagged as unusual activity.
- **If you suspect your account or personal data may have been compromised**, or if the standard reset process doesn't resolve your issue, contact PhilHealth through its official channels listed on www.philhealth.gov.ph rather than a number or link found elsewhere online.
- **Keep your registered email address current.** Since the password reset process depends entirely on that email, an outdated address is the most common reason members get locked out with no self-service way back in.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What information do I need for a PhilHealth portal password reset?**
Your 12-digit PIN and the email address registered to your Member Portal account — that's what the official Forgot Password form asks for.

**Is the password reset link sent by SMS or only by email?**
Based on the official Forgot Password page, the reset link is sent by email. The page does not mention an SMS option for this specific step.

**What if I forgot my PIN, not just my password?**
That's a different issue from a password reset. A forgotten PIN requires identity verification with PhilHealth directly, since PIN recovery isn't part of the Forgot Password form (which requires the PIN as an input).

**Can I just create a new Member Portal account instead of resetting my password?**
This guide does not recommend that as a workaround, since your PIN is tied to your existing PhilHealth records, and unofficial guides describing "workarounds" are not sourced from PhilHealth's official process. If Forgot Password doesn't resolve your access issue, contact PhilHealth rather than attempting to register again.

**Is there a fee for resetting my PhilHealth portal password?**
The official Forgot Password page does not indicate any fee — it only requires your PIN, registered email, and a CAPTCHA. Treat any request for payment tied to a "password reset" as a red flag.

**How long does it take to receive the reset email?**
The official page does not publish a specific delivery time. If you don't see it within a few minutes, check spam before assuming something went wrong.
