# PhilHealth PRN Online: What It's Actually Called in 2026 and How to Get It

If you're searching for a "PhilHealth PRN online," here's the most important thing to know upfront: PhilHealth's own official advisories and circulars do not use the term "PRN" (Payment Reference Number). The document you're actually looking for — the one that shows your reference number and amount due before you pay — is called the **Statement of Premium Account (SPA)**. This guide explains what the SPA is, how to get it online, and why it now matters more than ever for certain members in 2026.

This is written for individual PhilHealth members — particularly **Self-Paying Members** (self-earning individuals, professional practitioners, and most Overseas Filipinos) — who need a payment reference before contributing online or at a payment counter.

## Why "PRN" and "SPA" Get Confused

Many online guides and blogs refer to a "Payment Reference Number" or "PRN" when describing PhilHealth's online payment process. Based on a review of PhilHealth's official advisories, circulars, and website, PhilHealth's own documents consistently use a different term: the **Statement of Premium Account (SPA)**. PhilHealth Circular No. 2022-0021 describes the SPA as a system-generated billing statement showing the reference number and total premium due for a covered period, including any applicable interest or discount.

In short: if you're searching for a "PRN," what you likely need is your **SPA** — it serves the same practical purpose (a reference tied to your payment) but is PhilHealth's actual, current terminology.

## What Is the SPA?

The SPA is PhilHealth's official billing statement for premium contributions. It shows:

- Your PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN) or employer number
- The reference number tied to your payment
- The total premium amount due for the covered period, including applicable interest or discount where relevant

You present this SPA — often as a QR code — when paying through an Accredited Collecting Agent (ACA), a PhilHealth office, or certain online payment channels.

## The 2026 Change: "No SPA, No Payment"

Under PhilHealth Advisory No. 2026-0016, starting **April 1, 2026**, Self-Paying Members are required to secure and present their SPA before paying premium contributions. Payment transactions through any available payment channel will only be processed if an SPA accompanies them.

**Who this applies to**, per the advisory:

- Self-Earning Individuals
- Professional Practitioners
- Overseas Filipinos — **except** Sea-Based Migrant Workers

**How PhilHealth generates it:** The advisory states that PhilHealth will automatically generate the SPA for Self-Paying Members starting with the applicable month of January 2026 onward, and will send the SPA (or a notification of it) to the member's registered contact number or email address.

If your contact details on file are outdated, you may not receive this automatic notification — see the section below on keeping your information current.

## How to Get Your SPA Online: Step-by-Step

Based on PhilHealth's official advisory, there are two main online channels:

### Option 1: Through the PhilHealth Member Portal

1. Go to the PhilHealth Member Portal at memberinquiry.philhealth.gov.ph/member/ (reachable also through Online Services on www.philhealth.gov.ph).
2. Register for an account if you don't already have one, using your PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN).
3. Log in with your PIN and password.
4. Access, view, and download your SPA from your account, where it is available alongside your membership information and posted contributions.
5. From the portal, you can also pay your premium contribution directly using a credit/debit card, GCash, or Maya, according to the official advisory.

### Option 2: Through the SPA Generator

1. Go to spagen.philhealth.gov.ph, or scan the SPA Generator QR code displayed at Accredited Collecting Agents, PhilHealth Regional Offices, or Local Health Insurance Offices.
2. Follow the on-screen steps to generate your SPA.
3. Save or screenshot the generated QR code on your phone.
4. Present the screenshot to the teller or cashier at an Accredited Collecting Agent, or fill out the payment slip available at the counter before paying, and keep a copy of the payment slip as proof of payment.

## What You Can Do Through the Member Portal (Confirmed List)

According to PhilHealth's official 2026 advisory, the Member Portal lets you:

- Register to the National Health Insurance Program (NHIP)
- View your membership information and dependents
- Update or amend your membership record
- Check your posted premium contributions
- Assign yourself and your dependents to your preferred YAKAP provider
- Access, view, and download your SPA
- Pay your premium contributions using credit/debit card, GCash, or Maya

## Keeping Your Details Updated

Because PhilHealth sends the automatically generated SPA to your registered contact number or email, the advisory specifically recommends updating your personal details — including monthly income, contact number, address, and email address — through a PhilHealth Regional Office, Local Health Insurance Office, or the Member Portal, to make sure you actually receive it.

## Common Problems

- **You never received your SPA notification.** Your registered contact number or email may be outdated. Update it through the Member Portal or a PhilHealth office, then generate your SPA manually through the SPA Generator in the meantime.
- **A payment center or ACA is rejecting your payment.** As of April 1, 2026, Self-Paying Members generally cannot pay without a valid, accompanying SPA — generate one first.
- **You have unpaid past-due contributions (arrears).** Based on available official information, the SPA Generator is intended for current and upcoming period payments; for arrears or older unpaid premiums, visiting a PhilHealth office directly is the safer next step, since the online SPA Generator's coverage of overdue periods isn't clearly specified in the materials reviewed.
- **You're an employed member, not a Self-Paying Member.** The April 2026 "No SPA, No Payment" rule specifically targets Self-Paying Members. Employed members' contributions are typically remitted by their employer through a separate employer-side system.

## What to Do If Something Goes Wrong

- Keep every payment slip, SPA copy, and transaction confirmation you receive — these are your proof of payment if a contribution doesn't post correctly.
- If your SPA doesn't generate, doesn't match your expected amount, or a payment isn't accepted, contact PhilHealth directly rather than guessing:
  - PhilHealth Action Center: (02) 8662-2588
  - Smart subscribers: 0998-857-2957 or 0968-865-4670
  - Globe subscribers: 0917-127-5987 or 0917-110-9812
  - Email: actioncenter@philhealth.gov.ph
- You may also visit any PhilHealth Regional Office or Local Health Insurance Office for in-person assistance.
- Never share your SPA, QR code, PIN, or portal password with unauthorized individuals or third-party "processing" services — PhilHealth has publicly reminded members to transact only with authorized collecting agents.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is "PRN" and "SPA" the same thing?**
Functionally, both terms describe a payment reference used before completing a PhilHealth premium payment. However, PhilHealth's own official materials use "Statement of Premium Account (SPA)," not "PRN" — so if you were told to find a "PRN," you're looking for your SPA.

**Do I need an SPA if I'm an employed member?**
The April 2026 "No SPA, No Payment" policy specifically covers Self-Paying Members — self-earning individuals, professional practitioners, and Overseas Filipinos (excluding sea-based migrant workers). Employed members' premium remittance generally goes through their employer, so the details differ; check with your employer's HR or payroll team if you're unsure.

**Can I generate my SPA without registering a Member Portal account?**
Yes — the SPA Generator (spagen.philhealth.gov.ph) is available as a separate channel from the Member Portal. However, registering a Member Portal account also lets you view, download, and pay your SPA directly, along with checking your other membership records.

**What happens if I pay without an SPA after April 1, 2026?**
Per the official advisory, payment transactions in any available payment channel will only be processed with an accompanying SPA for covered Self-Paying Members — so a payment attempt without one may not be accepted.

**Where can I get help if my SPA problem isn't resolved online?**
Contact the PhilHealth Action Center at (02) 8662-2588, the Smart/Globe mobile hotlines listed above, or visit your nearest PhilHealth Regional Office or Local Health Insurance Office.
