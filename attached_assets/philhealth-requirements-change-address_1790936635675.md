# PhilHealth Requirements to Change Address: What to Prepare and How to Update (2026)

To change your address in PhilHealth, you generally update your **Member Data Record (MDR)** by submitting PhilHealth's member registration form (PMRF) with your new address and a valid ID. ## What Does "Change Address" Mean in PhilHealth?

Your **Member Data Record (MDR)** is the official record PhilHealth keeps about you. It includes your name, birthdate, civil status, dependents, and **contact details**, which include your address. A change of address means updating those details so PhilHealth has your current information.

This is a record update, not a new registration. Do not register again, because duplicate records can cause problems later.

## Why Update Your Address?

- PhilHealth communications can reach you if they need to contact you. **[VERIFY how PhilHealth uses member addresses]**
- Your records stay consistent with your employer's records and other documents.
- It can make future transactions, such as claims or membership updates, go more smoothly.

The available official source does not clearly specify a legal deadline for updating your address, so this guide does not state one.

## Who Needs to Update Their Address?

You may want to update your PhilHealth record if:

- You moved to a new home, city, or province
- Your address was recorded incorrectly when you registered
- You are an OFW and your Philippine contact address changed
- Your employer reports a new address for you

## Common Problems

- **Old address still appears.** The update may still be processing, or the form may have been incomplete.
- **Wrong office.** Some transactions may need to be filed at a specific office. Ask which office handles your record. **[VERIFY]**
- **Incomplete address.** Include the house or unit number, street, barangay, city or municipality, province, and postal code if known.
- **Duplicate records.** If you suspect you have more than one record or PIN, ask PhilHealth to review it instead of registering again.
- **Employer records differ.** Ask HR to align their records with your updated details.

## What To Do If Something Goes Wrong

1. Keep copies of everything you submitted and any acknowledgment slip.
2. Check your MDR through official PhilHealth channels.
3. Visit the PhilHealth office or Local Health Insurance Office that handles your record and ask for the status.
4. Use the official hotline or email channels listed on the PhilHealth website. Avoid numbers from unofficial pages.
5. If you suspect your record was changed without your knowledge, report it to PhilHealth through official channels.
