The PhilHealth portal is PhilHealth's online platform where registered members can manage parts of their membership without visiting a branch office in person. Depending on what's currently enabled on the system, this can include:

- Viewing your Member Data Record (MDR)
- Checking your contribution/payment history
- Updating certain personal information
- Printing or downloading membership documents
- Accessing other online member services **[VERIFY: full current list of self-service features, as PhilHealth has changed and consolidated online systems over time]**

## Who Can Use the Portal?

Generally, any registered PhilHealth member with a valid PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN) can create and use an online account, including:

- Employed members
- Self-paying (individually paying) members
- Voluntary members
- Overseas Filipino Workers (OFWs)
- Other member categories recognized by PhilHealth

## What You Need Before You Log In

Before attempting to log in, have the following ready:

- **Your PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN)** — found on your Member Data Record or PhilHealth ID/documents.

- A stable internet connection and an updated browser.
