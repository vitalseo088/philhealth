## PhilHealth Contribution Online: How to Pay and Check It in 2026

If you need to pay or check your PhilHealth contribution online, you can do this through the official **PhilHealth Member Portal** at memberinquiry.philhealth.gov.ph. This guide is for employed, self-employed, voluntary, and OFW members who want to understand the current 2026 contribution rate, generate a payment reference online, and confirm that their contributions are posted correctly. It does not cover employer remittance through the Electronic Premium Remittance System (EPRS), which uses a separate login.

### Quick Answer

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| 2026 premium rate | 5% of monthly basic salary or declared income |
| Salary floor | ₱10,000 (minimum monthly premium: ₱500) |
| Salary ceiling | ₱100,000 (maximum monthly premium: ₱5,000) |
| Employed members | Split equally — 2.5% employee, 2.5% employer |
| Self-paying, voluntary, OFW members | Pay the full 5% themselves |
| Where to pay/check online | PhilHealth Member Portal: memberinquiry.philhealth.gov.ph |
| Online payment channels | GCash, debit/credit card, and other PhilHealth-accredited digital channels |
| Payment frequency options | Monthly, quarterly, semi-annually, or annually |

According to a PhilHealth advisory reported by the Philippine Information Agency in May 2026, the premium contribution rate for 2026 stays at 5 percent of a member's monthly basic income, which PhilHealth describes as the final scheduled step under the Universal Health Care (UHC) Law (Republic Act No. 11223). Under that schedule, a member earning ₱10,000 a month pays a fixed ₱500 monthly premium, someone earning between ₱10,000.01 and ₱99,999.99 pays 5% of their monthly income, and members earning ₱100,000 or more pay the maximum of ₱5,000 a month.

### What Is the PhilHealth Member Portal?

The Member Portal is PhilHealth's official self-service website for individual members. Once you register and activate an account there, you can view your Membership Data Record (MDR), check your posted contributions, and — for self-paying, voluntary, and OFW members — generate a payment reference and pay online instead of visiting a branch. Employers do not use this portal; they remit contributions separately through EPRS using a PhilHealth Employer Number (PEN).

A safety note worth repeating from PhilHealth's own public guidance: only use the official domain, **philhealth.gov.ph** (and its member subdomain, memberinquiry.philhealth.gov.ph). Several unofficial sites mimic the PhilHealth portal, so double-check the URL before entering your PIN or password.

### Who Can Pay Their Contribution Online?

- **Self-paying / voluntary members** — freelancers, self-employed individuals, and other members who are not covered by an employer.
- **OFWs** — land-based and sea-based overseas Filipino workers, who can settle contributions from abroad through the same portal.
- **Employed members** — you can log in to *check* that your employer's remittances are posted, but the actual payment is deducted from your salary and remitted by your employer, not paid by you individually online.

### 2026 Contribution Rate: How It's Calculated

**Formula:**
Monthly basic salary (or declared monthly income) × 5% = Total monthly premium

For **employed members**, that total is split 50/50 between employee and employer (2.5% each), and the employer is responsible for deducting the employee's share and remitting the combined amount.

For **self-paying, voluntary, and OFW members**, the computation is based on declared monthly income, and the member is responsible for the full 5% themselves.

**Worked examples** *(for illustration only — always confirm your actual bracket against the current official schedule)*:

| Monthly Salary/Income | Total Premium (5%) | Employed: Employee Share | Employed: Employer Share | Self-Paying/OFW: Full Amount |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ₱10,000 or below | ₱500 (floor) | ₱250 | ₱250 | ₱500 |
| ₱20,000 | ₱1,000 | ₱500 | ₱500 | ₱1,000 |
| ₱35,000 | ₱1,750 | ₱875 | ₱875 | ₱1,750 |
| ₱100,000 or above | ₱5,000 (ceiling) | ₱2,500 | ₱2,500 | ₱5,000 |

A note on conflicting figures online: while the great majority of current sources — including the government-run Philippine Information Agency — confirm the 2026 rate at **5%**, a few unofficial blogs and portal-lookalike sites currently list a **5.5%** rate. That figure could not be verified against any official PhilHealth advisory or circular, so treat any site quoting 5.5% for 2026 with caution and cross-check your actual deduction against your payslip or your Member Portal contribution history.

### How to Pay Your PhilHealth Contribution Online (Self-Paying, Voluntary, and OFW Members)

1. **Go to the official Member Portal.** Visit memberinquiry.philhealth.gov.ph and log in with your 12-digit PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN) and password. If you don't have an account yet, register first and activate it through the confirmation sent to your email.
2. **Open Payment Management.** From your dashboard, go to the payment or "Payment Management" section.
3. **Generate a Statement of Premium Account (SPA).** This document/reference shows how much you owe and for which coverage period. Select how many months you want to pay for (monthly, quarterly, semi-annually, or annually).
4. **Review the payment summary.** Confirm the amount and coverage period are correct before proceeding.
5. **Choose your payment method and pay.** You'll typically be routed to PhilHealth's accredited online collection partner, where you can pay via GCash, debit or credit card, or another supported digital channel.
6. **Keep your reference number and receipt.** Save or screenshot your payment confirmation in case you need to follow up on a posting delay.

### How to Check Your PhilHealth Contribution Online

1. Log in to the Member Portal at memberinquiry.philhealth.gov.ph.
2. Go to the contributions or "Member Inquiry" section of your dashboard.
3. Review the list of posted months. Look for gaps — a missing month usually means a payment hasn't been posted yet, or wasn't remitted.
4. If you need proof of active membership (for example, before a hospital admission), go to the MDR section and download or print your Membership Data Record, which lists your contribution history along with your registered dependents.

### Common Problems

- **A recent payment isn't showing yet.** Online postings can take time to reflect, especially soon after payment. Give it a reasonable processing window before assuming there's an error.
- **Employer contributions have gaps.** If you're employed and see missing months, this is a matter to raise with your employer's HR or payroll team first, since they are responsible for deducting and remitting your share.
- **"Account not found" when logging in.** Your PhilHealth PIN and your Member Portal account are two different things — having a PIN doesn't automatically mean you have portal access. You may need to complete portal registration separately.
- **Suspected phishing site.** If a payment or login page doesn't sit on the philhealth.gov.ph domain, stop and don't enter your credentials or payment details.

### What to Do If Something Goes Wrong

- **Missing or unposted payment:** Keep your payment receipt or reference number and be ready to present it if you follow up.
- **Employer remittance issue:** Raise it with your HR/payroll department; employers are required to remit both shares on your behalf.
- **Portal access issues or unresolved discrepancies:** Contact PhilHealth directly through its official hotline or nearest Local Health Insurance Office (LHIO) rather than a third-party site, and verify any contact details through philhealth.gov.ph before using them, since PhilHealth's published hotline and email details can change.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Is the PhilHealth contribution rate still increasing every year?**
For 2026, multiple current reports — including PhilHealth's own advisory as reported by the Philippine Information Agency — describe 5% as the final scheduled rate under the UHC Law's phased schedule, with no further automatic increase currently in effect. Always check the latest PhilHealth advisory, since this could change with new legislation or a new circular.

**Can I pay more than one month at a time online?**
Yes. The Member Portal's payment/SPA generation step lets self-paying, voluntary, and OFW members select monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, or annual coverage before paying.

**Do employed members need to pay online themselves?**
No. Your share is deducted from your salary and remitted by your employer together with the employer's share. You can still use the portal to check that those contributions are posted.

**What if I don't know my monthly income for self-paying computation?**
Contributions for self-paying members are computed based on declared monthly income. If you're unsure how to classify or declare your income, it's best to confirm directly with PhilHealth rather than guess, since this can affect your posted premium.

**Is GCash the only online payment option?**
No. Reporting on the Member Portal describes multiple online options for self-paying members, including GCash, debit and credit cards, and other PhilHealth-accredited digital payment partners.
