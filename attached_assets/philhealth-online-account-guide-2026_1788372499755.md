## What Is a PhilHealth Online Account?

A PhilHealth online account is your personal, password-protected access point to PhilHealth's member-facing online system. Once set up, it generally lets you:

- View your Member Data Record (MDR)
- Check your contribution and payment history
- Update certain personal information
- Download or print membership-related documents
- Access other self-service features currently offered **[VERIFY: the complete, current list of features — this has changed as PhilHealth has updated its online systems]**

It's different from simply having a PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN). Every member has a PIN once registered with PhilHealth, but that alone doesn't give you online access — you have to separately create and verify an online account.

## Who Can Create a PhilHealth Online Account?

Generally, any individual who is already a registered PhilHealth member with a valid PIN can create an online account, including:

- Employed members
- Self-paying (individually paying) members — members who pay their own contributions rather than through an employer
- Voluntary members
- OFWs (Overseas Filipino Workers)
- Other recognized member categories

Dependents typically don't get their own separate online account; their information is usually viewed through the principal member's account. **[VERIFY: current rules, if any, on separate dependent access]**
