# PhilHealth Accredited Dental Clinic: How to Find and Use One (2026 Guide)

A PhilHealth-accredited dental clinic is a facility PhilHealth has approved to deliver its Preventive Oral Health Services Package — the benefit that covers routine dental check-ups, cleaning, fluoride treatment, and select restorative procedures. This guide explains what's covered, how the accreditation and referral system works, and how to find or verify an accredited clinic.

This guide is for PhilHealth members who want to use their dental benefit and need to know which clinics they can actually use it at. It reflects PhilHealth Circular No. 2024-0034, which introduced the Preventive Oral Health Services Package as part of primary care, effective from its December 2024 issuance, along with PhilHealth's more recent guidance on claims coding. Note that PhilHealth's full circular text was not available as machine-readable text in the source checked for this article, so some figures below are drawn from PhilHealth's official claims-coding advisory together with consistent secondary reporting on the circular's rollout — this is flagged where relevant.

## What Is the PhilHealth Dental Benefit?

PhilHealth's dental coverage is formally called the **Preventive Oral Health Services Package**, established under **PhilHealth Circular No. 2024-0034** (published December 28, 2024). It's delivered as part of PhilHealth's primary care system — the same system now organized under YAKAP (Yaman ng Kalusugan Program).

Based on PhilHealth's official claims-coding advisory (PhilHealth Advisory No. 2025-0075), the package's first-visit mandatory services are billed under a specific package code, **OPH01**, at a rate of **₱300**, and cover:

- Mouth examination/oral screening
- Oral prophylaxis (cleaning)
- Fluoride varnish application

Beyond this confirmed first-visit rate, PhilHealth's own board announcements and consistent secondary reporting on the circular describe the package as also covering pit and fissure sealant application or Class V restoration procedures, for up to two teeth per beneficiary per year, along with emergency tooth extractions when needed. Total annual coverage under the package has been widely reported at up to ₱1,000 per beneficiary per year. Because the full circular text could not be independently confirmed as machine-readable in the source checked for this article, readers should treat the ₱300 first-visit rate (from PhilHealth's own advisory) as confirmed, and the additional service details and the ₱1,000 annual figure as PhilHealth's publicly announced package design pending direct confirmation from the full circular text.

## What Is a PhilHealth Accredited Dental Clinic?

A PhilHealth-accredited dental clinic is a dental facility that PhilHealth has formally approved to deliver the Preventive Oral Health Services Package and be paid the corresponding package rate. Based on PhilHealth's board announcements on this benefit, there are two routes by which dental care is delivered under this package:

1. **Dentists working within a primary care/YAKAP provider** that has dental services as part of its offering.
2. **Stand-alone accredited dentists**, who see patients specifically through a **referral** from a registered primary care/YAKAP provider.

This structure means accreditation for this benefit is tied to a facility's specific listing as a Preventive Oral Health Benefit Package Provider — being a general dental clinic, or even being PhilHealth-accredited for some other purpose, does not automatically mean a clinic is accredited for this specific dental package.

## How to Verify or Find a PhilHealth Accredited Dental Clinic

### Step 1: Check PhilHealth's Published Provider List

PhilHealth periodically publishes a downloadable **List of Accredited Preventive Oral Health Benefit Package Providers**, organized by region and updated on a rolling basis, showing facility name, contact details, address, and accreditation expiry date. This is the most direct official source for confirming whether a specific facility is accredited for this benefit.

### Step 2: Use PhilHealth's Online Health Facilities Accreditation Portal

PhilHealth's official Online Services page includes a Health Facilities accreditation section, described by PhilHealth as showing the complete list of health facilities and their accreditation status — useful for cross-checking a facility you're considering.

### Step 3: Ask Your YAKAP Clinic for a Referral

If your registered YAKAP Clinic doesn't have dental services on-site, ask them directly whether they can refer you to a stand-alone accredited dentist for the Preventive Oral Health Services Package, since a referral is how members generally reach a stand-alone accredited dentist under this structure.

### Step 4: Confirm Accreditation Directly With the Clinic

Ask the dental clinic to show its current PhilHealth accreditation specifically for the Preventive Oral Health Benefit Package — not just general PhilHealth participation — since accreditation is benefit-specific.

## How to Use the Dental Benefit: Step-by-Step

1. **Be registered and empaneled with a YAKAP Clinic.** This is generally the entry point into PhilHealth's primary care system, of which the dental benefit is a part.
2. **Check if your YAKAP Clinic offers dental services directly.** If it does, you can be seen there.
3. **If not, ask for a referral to a stand-alone accredited dentist.** Present this referral at the accredited dental clinic.
4. **Undergo the covered preventive services** — mouth examination, cleaning, and fluoride application — at the accredited facility.
5. **Ask about further covered procedures if needed,** such as pit and fissure sealants or Class V restoration, subject to the reported two-teeth-per-year limit and the facility's own assessment of your case.

## Common Problems

- **"The dental clinic near me says it accepts PhilHealth, but I'm not sure it's accredited for this specific package."** Ask to see their accreditation specifically for the Preventive Oral Health Benefit Package, and cross-check against PhilHealth's published provider list for that package.
- **"I was asked to pay for services I thought were free."** Ask the clinic to clarify which services fall within the PhilHealth package rate and which, if any, are outside it — PhilHealth coverage depends on the applicable benefit package and its specific rules, not a blanket "everything free" guarantee.
- **"My YAKAP Clinic won't refer me to a dentist."** Referrals are generally needed to access a stand-alone accredited dentist under this structure; if your clinic is unable to provide one, raise the matter with PhilHealth directly.
- **"I can't find my clinic on PhilHealth's published list."** Lists are updated periodically and organized by region — check the most recent version and confirm the clinic's exact registered name, since name mismatches are a common source of confusion.

## What to Do If Something Goes Wrong

If you have trouble finding an accredited dental clinic, verifying accreditation, or resolving a billing issue related to this benefit, PhilHealth's general contact channels include:

- Calling the PhilHealth hotline
- Emailing actioncenter@philhealth.gov.ph
- Visiting any PhilHealth office or your nearest Local Health Insurance Office (LHIO)
- Using the click-to-call feature on the official PhilHealth website

Always verify current contact numbers directly on philhealth.gov.ph, since these can be updated.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is the PhilHealth dental benefit available to all members?**
PhilHealth has described the Preventive Oral Health Services Package as part of its primary care benefits generally available to registered beneficiaries; confirm your specific eligibility with your YAKAP Clinic or PhilHealth directly.

**Can I go straight to a dental clinic without visiting my YAKAP Clinic first?**
Based on PhilHealth's described structure, a stand-alone accredited dentist is generally reached through a referral from a primary care/YAKAP provider, rather than as a walk-in benefit on its own — confirm with your specific clinic.

**Does the dental benefit cover major procedures like root canals or extractions of healthy teeth?**
The available official information for this benefit describes coverage focused on preventive services (examination, cleaning, fluoride) plus limited restorative work (sealants, Class V, up to two teeth per year) and emergency tooth extractions when necessary. It is not described as a comprehensive dental-treatment benefit — confirm what a specific procedure would fall under with your provider.

**How often can I use this benefit?**
PhilHealth's own announcements describe this as an annual routine preventive benefit; specific frequency limits for each component were not independently confirmed in the source checked for this article beyond the reported two-teeth-per-year limit on sealant/Class V procedures. Confirm current frequency rules with your provider or PhilHealth.

**Where can I find the official list of accredited dental clinics?**
PhilHealth publishes a downloadable List of Accredited Preventive Oral Health Benefit Package Providers on its website, organized by region and updated periodically, alongside its online Health Facilities accreditation-status portal.
