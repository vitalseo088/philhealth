## What Does "PhilHealth Accredited" Mean?

**Accreditation** means a health facility or professional has been officially recognized by PhilHealth as qualified to provide services covered by PhilHealth benefits. Many PhilHealth benefits are paid to **accredited** providers, which is why accreditation matters before you book a service.

A facility may be accredited for some services and not others. For example, being accredited as a hospital does not automatically tell you which eye procedures are covered. **[VERIFY how PhilHealth records service-specific accreditation]**

## How to Check If an Eye Clinic Is PhilHealth Accredited

1. **Open the official PhilHealth provider directory.** Use PhilHealth's official website or official channels. **[VERIFY exact directory name and how to access it]**
2. **Search by facility name or location.** Try different spellings, since facilities may be listed under a registered name that differs from the signage.
3. **Check the provider type and status.** Look for the facility type and accreditation status shown by PhilHealth.
4. **Note the date.** If the directory shows an update date, record it. If it does not, treat the information as possibly outdated.
5. **Confirm with PhilHealth.** If you cannot find the clinic, call or visit the PhilHealth office using official contact details from the PhilHealth website.
6. **Confirm with the facility.** Ask the clinic's PhilHealth desk or billing staff whether they are accredited for your specific service.

## What Does PhilHealth Cover for Eye Care?

PhilHealth coverage depends on the applicable benefit package, your eligibility, and the facility's accreditation. This guide does not diagnose conditions or say whether any treatment is appropriate for you. Your doctor makes medical decisions.

What this guide can say in general terms:

- Hospital-based services are generally paid to accredited health facilities through **case rates**, and the benefit is deducted from your bill under the applicable rules. This means PhilHealth does not simply pay the entire bill.

## Common Problems

- **Clinic says "PhilHealth accepted" but is not listed.** Verify with PhilHealth. Do not rely on signage or advertisements alone.
- **Facility accredited, but service not covered.** Accreditation does not mean every service is covered. Ask about the specific benefit package.
- **Membership not showing as active.** Check your MDR and contributions. See the related guides below.
- **Dependent not on record.** Update your dependents before the visit.
- **Directory outdated or confusing.** Contact PhilHealth for confirmation.

## What To Do If Something Goes Wrong

4. Visit the PhilHealth office or Local Health Insurance Office that handles your record.
