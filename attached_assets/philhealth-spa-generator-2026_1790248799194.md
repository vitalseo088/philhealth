## PhilHealth SPA Generator 2026: How to Generate Yours Before Paying

Here's the direct answer: since **April 1, 2026**, self-paying PhilHealth members are expected to generate a **Statement of Premium Account (SPA)** — a QR-coded billing statement — **before** paying their contribution, using the dedicated **SPA Generator at spagen.philhealth.gov.ph** or by scanning the QR code on PhilHealth's official website. This rolled out under **PhilHealth Advisory No. 2026-0016**, alongside a **"No SPA, No Payment" policy** for this member category.

This guide is for **self-paying members** — self-earning individuals, professional practitioners, and most Overseas Filipinos — who need to understand what the SPA Generator actually is, who has to use it, and how to generate one correctly in **2026**.

> **Important limitation:** the SPA Generator and the "No SPA, No Payment" policy apply specifically to **self-paying members**. If you're an **employed member**, your employer handles contribution reporting and payment through PhilHealth's separate Electronic Premium Remittance System (EPRS) — this article's process does not apply to you.

## What Is the PhilHealth SPA Generator?

The **Statement of Premium Account (SPA)** is a system-generated billing document that shows your **PIN, full name, membership type, the coverage months you're paying for, your computed contribution amount, and a unique SPA reference number** — all tied together in a **QR code** you present when paying. The **SPA Generator** is the online tool PhilHealth built specifically so self-paying members can produce this document themselves, ahead of making a payment, rather than paying first and hoping the record matches up afterward.

This isn't a minor convenience feature — it's part of a broader shift established under **PhilHealth Circular No. 2026-0011**, which set out that self-paying members would need to present an SPA for most payment transactions starting in 2026, with **2026 treated as a transition year** before the requirement becomes strictly mandatory on **January 1, 2027**. The SPA Generator at spagen.philhealth.gov.ph, launched under **PhilHealth Advisory No. 2026-0016**, is the specific tool that operationalizes this requirement, effective **April 1, 2026**.

## Why PhilHealth Introduced This

Per PhilHealth's own stated rationale, requiring an SPA before payment **"reduces errors in payment processing and helps members maintain accurate, up-to-date contribution records, which are essential in availing of PhilHealth benefits."** In practice, this addresses a long-standing problem: payments made without a clear reference number are harder for PhilHealth's system to match correctly to the right member and the right coverage period, which is a common cause of contributions appearing "missing" even though the money was actually paid.

## Who Needs to Use the SPA Generator

Based on PhilHealth's own advisory, the "No SPA, No Payment" policy applies to **self-paying members**, specifically:

- **Self-Earning Individuals**
- **Professional Practitioners**
- **Overseas Filipinos**, excluding sea-based migrant workers (seafarers generally follow a separate arrangement)

**Employed members do not use this tool.** Their contributions are reported and remitted by their employer through PhilHealth's Electronic Premium Remittance System (EPRS), a separate employer-facing system with its own SPA generation process.

## How to Generate Your SPA: Step by Step

1. **Go to spagen.philhealth.gov.ph**, or scan the SPA QR code available on PhilHealth's official website.
2. **Log in or verify your identity** using your PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN) and the details the system requires.
3. **Select the applicable coverage period(s)** you intend to pay for.
4. **Review the computed premium amount** shown, which is based on your declared/reported income and the number of months selected.
5. **Generate the SPA.** The system produces a QR-coded document containing your PIN, name, membership type, coverage period, amount due, and a unique SPA reference number.
6. **Take the SPA to your chosen payment channel** — an online payment gateway (GCash, Maya, online banking) or an over-the-counter accredited collecting agent — and use it to complete your payment.
7. **Keep a copy of the SPA and your payment confirmation** together as your proof of the transaction.

**If you already have an active, unpaid SPA for the same period**, the system is generally expected not to generate a new one until that existing SPA is either paid or cancelled — check your Member Portal or the SPA Generator itself if you're unable to create a new one and suspect this is the reason.

## What to Do If Your SPA Is Missing or Incorrect

If your SPA doesn't reflect the correct period, amount, or your current details, PhilHealth Circular No. 2026-0011 specifies that a self-paying member may **generate a corrected SPA through the Member Portal or the SPA generator, or request an adjustment directly from a PhilHealth office.** Don't attempt to work around an incorrect SPA by paying an unrelated amount — the reference number is what links your payment to your record, so paying against a wrong or outdated SPA can itself become a source of the exact posting problems this tool is meant to prevent.

## The 2026 Transition Period

PhilHealth has acknowledged that 2026 is a **transition year** for this requirement — the corporation has indicated it will allow **limited, documented flexibility** for situations such as first-time SPA users encountering system issues, or members with pending record verification, before the requirement becomes strictly enforced. **Full mandatory implementation is scheduled for January 1, 2027.** If you're a self-paying member encountering a genuine technical obstacle in 2026 while trying to generate or use an SPA, that context is worth raising directly with a PhilHealth office rather than assuming you have no options.

## The GIDA Exception

The SPA requirement carries one documented exception: payments made through **designated collecting agents located in Geographically Isolated and Disadvantaged Areas (GIDAs)** are not subject to the same "No SPA, No Payment" enforcement as the general rule, reflecting the practical realities of accessing PhilHealth's digital tools in some parts of the country.

## Common Problems and What to Do

**You can't generate a new SPA.**
Check whether you already have an active, unpaid SPA for the same period — the system generally won't create a duplicate. Pay or cancel the existing one first.

**Your SPA shows the wrong amount or period.**
Generate a corrected SPA through the Member Portal or the SPA Generator, or request an adjustment from a PhilHealth office directly, rather than paying against an SPA you know is inaccurate.

**A collecting agent won't accept your payment without an SPA.**
This is consistent with the "No SPA, No Payment" policy for self-paying members effective April 1, 2026 — generate your SPA first through spagen.philhealth.gov.ph or the Member Portal before attempting to pay.

**You're in a remote area with limited access to generate an SPA online.**
Ask whether the collecting agent you're using is a designated GIDA agent, since these are exempted from the standard SPA enforcement under the broader framework.

**You paid without an SPA before the policy was enforced and now aren't sure how your older payments are tracked.**
Contributions paid before this requirement took effect follow the posting and verification process that applied at the time; if you're unsure whether an older payment posted correctly, check your Contribution History in the Member Portal and raise a discrepancy with PhilHealth if something looks off.

**You're an employed member and got told you need an SPA — that doesn't sound right.**
Correct — the SPA Generator and "No SPA, No Payment" policy apply to self-paying members. Employed members' contributions go through their employer's EPRS process instead. If you were told otherwise, clarify your membership category with PhilHealth directly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the PhilHealth SPA Generator?**
An online tool at spagen.philhealth.gov.ph that lets self-paying members generate a QR-coded Statement of Premium Account before making a payment, so the payment is automatically linked to the correct record.

**Do I need to use the SPA Generator if I'm an employed member?**
No — employed members' contributions are reported and remitted by their employer through PhilHealth's Electronic Premium Remittance System (EPRS), a separate process.

**When did the "No SPA, No Payment" policy start?**
It took effect April 1, 2026, under PhilHealth Advisory No. 2026-0016, for self-paying members.

**Is this requirement fully enforced right now, or is there flexibility?**
PhilHealth has described 2026 as a transition year with some documented flexibility for genuine issues (like first-time technical problems), with full mandatory enforcement scheduled for January 1, 2027.

**Can I still pay without an SPA in a remote area?**
Designated collecting agents in Geographically Isolated and Disadvantaged Areas (GIDAs) are exempted from the standard SPA enforcement.

**What if my SPA has the wrong amount or wrong coverage period?**
You can generate a corrected SPA through the Member Portal or the SPA Generator, or request an adjustment directly from a PhilHealth office.

**Where exactly do I go to generate my SPA?**
spagen.philhealth.gov.ph, or by scanning the QR code available on PhilHealth's official website. It's also accessible inside the PhilHealth Member Portal under Payment Management.
