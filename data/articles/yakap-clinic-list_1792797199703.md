# YAKAP Clinic List 2026: How to Find and Check an Accredited Clinic

PhilHealth publishes the official **YAKAP clinic list** as a PDF titled *List of Accredited YAKAP Clinics for CY 2026*. The version we reviewed is marked **"Updated as of September 30, 2026"** and runs to 158 pages. It lists accredited facilities by region and province or city, with each facility's contact details, accreditation expiry date and a government or private marker.

This guide is for PhilHealth members and dependents who want to find a clinic or confirm that a clinic they are considering is accredited. We do not reproduce the full list here, because it changes. Use the official PDF, and check it again before you choose or visit a clinic. This article does not cover benefit amounts and is not medical advice.

## What Is the YAKAP Clinic List?

YAKAP stands for *Yaman ng Kalusugan Program*, PhilHealth's primary care benefit. To use it, you select an **accredited** YAKAP clinic, complete a First Patient Encounter (FPE), and sign the YAKAP Empanelment Slip. Under PhilHealth Advisory No. 2026-0023, empanelment to an unaccredited primary care facility is not valid YAKAP registration and is not covered for benefit entitlement, capitation payment or claim reimbursement. The accredited list is therefore the reference for whether a clinic qualifies.

The September 30, 2026 version has these columns:

| Column | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Name of health facility | The accredited facility, numbered in the list. |

## Step-by-Step: Find a YAKAP Clinic Near You

1. **Open the official PDF** from PhilHealth's accredited facilities page and confirm the "Updated as of" date at the top of each page.
2. **Jump to your region and province or city.** Use the headings, or use your find function and type your municipality.
3. **Note nearby options.** PhilHealth's standard is that your clinic should be within **30 km** of your declared home or office address. A farther clinic is allowed if it is your informed choice, but PhilHealth strongly discourages it (Advisory No. 2026-0023).
4. **Check the expiry date.** If the date is near, ask the clinic whether its accreditation is being renewed.
5. **Contact the clinic.** Use the number or email in the list to ask about your First Patient Encounter schedule, hours and any co-payment.
6. **Select the clinic officially.** Use the eGov PH Super App, the PhilHealth Member Portal, or a PhilHealth office (LHIO). Calling or visiting a clinic does not by itself register you. The circular describes these channels in detail.

## Things to Know Before You Choose From the List

### Check the expiry date

Accreditation periods vary. Many entries we saw show 12/31/2026, while others show 12/31/2027 or 12/31/2028. Since the current date is late October 2026, a clinic that shows 12/31/2026 may be waiting for renewal. We could not confirm from the official documents how renewals appear in later versions of the list, so ask the clinic.

### Public and private clinics may cost differently

Under PhilHealth Advisory No. 2026-0003, you should not be charged for covered services in public YAKAP clinics. A private YAKAP clinic may charge a co-payment, capped at ₱900 per member per year. Look at the G or P marker, and ask the clinic which applies.

### Not every entry is a typical neighborhood clinic

The list includes rural health units, district hospitals, private hospitals and clinics, and also other facility types. We saw laboratories and diagnostic centers, and institutional clinics such as school-division or agency clinics. PhilHealth's circular says workplace and school clinics can serve their own members through settings-based selection. Whether a given institutional clinic accepts the general public is not stated in the list. Confirm with the facility.

### Only fixed, permanent clinics qualify

PhilHealth says mobile health units, telemedicine-only providers and transient outreach teams cannot be accredited as stand-alone YAKAP clinics, and cannot be the basis of empanelment. A mobile service may only work as a supplemental provider of an accredited fixed clinic.

### Contact details in the list can be incomplete

Some entries have a missing telephone number or an email that looks mistyped. If a listed number does not work, try the email, contact your nearest LHIO, or call the PhilHealth Corporate Action Center at (02) 866-225-88 (the number given in Advisory No. 2026-0003).

### Medicines come from separate providers

PhilHealth also publishes a separate list, *Accredited PhilHealth GAMOT Package Providers for CY 2026*, which includes pharmacies. GAMOT is PhilHealth's outpatient medicines benefit. A pharmacy appearing on that list is a different kind of provider from a YAKAP clinic.

## Common Problems

- **The clinic you want is not on the list.** It may not be accredited, may be accredited under a slightly different name, or may be listed under a nearby municipality. Search by street or municipality. If you still cannot find it, ask the clinic for proof of YAKAP accreditation, or ask your LHIO.
- **The list looks out of date.** Always check the "Updated as of" date. A November 2025 listing for CY 2025 and the September 30, 2026 listing for CY 2026 are different documents. Use the newest one.
- **The clinic refuses to accept you.** PhilHealth's circular says a clinic cannot reject a member who selected it unless it has reached its declared beneficiary limit.
- **You picked a clinic and want to change it.** If you have not yet had your FPE, you can transfer at any time through your nearest LHIO. If you are already empaneled, you can transfer only in the fourth quarter of the year, effective January, unless the clinic closes or loses its accreditation.
- **No clinic within 30 km.** Advisory No. 2026-0023 says a supplemental circular would set rules for out-of-facility services in areas with no clinic within 30 km. We did not find that circular, so ask your PhilHealth Regional Office or LHIO what applies in your area.

## What To Do If Something Goes Wrong

1. **Contact the clinic** using the details in the list.
2. **Visit your nearest LHIO** for selection problems, record corrections or transfers.
3. **Contact the PhilHealth Corporate Action Center**, which Advisory No. 2026-0003 lists as available 24/7:
   - Call: (02) 866-225-88
   - Text: 0917-127-5987
   - Email: yakaptugon@philhealth.gov.ph
4. **Keep your records:** a screenshot of your clinic selection and the date of the list you checked.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where can I download the official YAKAP clinic list?
From PhilHealth's accredited facilities page. The file we reviewed was titled *List of Accredited YAKAP Clinics for CY 2026*, updated as of September 30, 2026.

### Does the list include private clinics?
Yes. The list includes both government and private facilities, marked with G or P.

### Is the YAKAP clinic list the same as the GAMOT list?
No. They are separate PhilHealth lists. The GAMOT list covers medicine package providers, including pharmacies.

### How often is the list updated?
The official documents we reviewed do not state an update schedule. We saw a CY 2025 list updated as of December 31, 2025 cited by other sites, and a CY 2026 list updated as of September 30, 2026. Check the date each time.

### Can I choose a clinic in another city?
Yes, but PhilHealth's standard is within 30 km of your declared home or office address. A farther clinic is allowed if it is your informed choice, though strongly discouraged.

### Can I use any clinic on the list without selecting it first?
No. You need to select the clinic, complete the FPE and sign the YAKAP Empanelment Slip. Being listed only means the clinic is accredited.

### Do I need to select a clinic for my dependents too?
Yes. In the Member Portal, you select a clinic for each dependent separately.

### Does the list say whether a clinic has open slots?
The official list does not show slots or availability. PhilHealth's circular says clinics declare how many beneficiaries they can accommodate.

### Is a clinic on the list guaranteed to offer every YAKAP service?
The list itself does not show the services a facility provides. Ask the clinic before you go.
