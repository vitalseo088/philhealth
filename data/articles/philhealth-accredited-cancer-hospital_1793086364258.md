# PhilHealth Accredited Cancer Hospital: How to Find and Check One in 2026

If you or a family member needs cancer care, the first thing to know is that **"PhilHealth accredited" is not one single list.** PhilHealth publishes separate lists for hospitals, standalone cancer treatment facilities, cancer screening providers, and facilities contracted for Z Benefit cancer packages. A hospital can appear on one list and not another.

This guide explains what each list means, shows the 2026 cancer treatment facilities PhilHealth currently lists, and walks you through checking a facility yourself before you go. It is written for PhilHealth members, dependents, and anyone helping a patient find care in the Philippines. It does not give medical advice. For treatment decisions, your doctor is the right person to ask.

> **Provider information can change. Check the official PhilHealth provider directory before visiting.**

## Quick Answer

PhilHealth's official accredited-facilities page, updated as of **September 30, 2026**, links to several lists that matter for cancer care:

| If you need... | Which official list to check |
|---|---|
| A standalone cancer center for treatment | **Cancer Treatment Facilities** list |
| A general hospital for admission or surgery | **Hospitals and Infirmaries/Dispensaries** list |
| Cancer screening tests (mammogram, colonoscopy, etc.) | **Cancer Screening Test Providers** list |
| Z Benefit package for breast, prostate, cervical, colon, rectal cancer or ALL | **Contracted Health Facilities → Z Benefit Package Providers** list |
| Primary care and a referral to screening | **YAKAP Clinics** list |

Start from PhilHealth's [Accredited Health Facilities page](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/partners/providers/facilities/accredited/) and its [Contracted Health Facilities page](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/partners/providers/facilities/contracted/).

## What "PhilHealth Accredited" Means for Cancer Care

**Accredited** means a health facility has met PhilHealth's requirements and is recognized to provide services for PhilHealth members. PhilHealth reviews accreditation on a yearly cycle. For example, its advisory on renewal for calendar year 2026 set an application window of October 1 to December 29, 2025 (PhilHealth Advisory No. 2025-0055).

**Contracted** is a separate status used for Z Benefit packages. Z Benefits are PhilHealth's special packages for certain serious illnesses. The contracted list for these packages was updated as of September 30, 2026 and includes these cancer packages: acute lymphocytic leukemia (ALL), breast, prostate, cervical, colon, and rectal cancer.

This matters because a facility being accredited as a hospital does not automatically mean it is contracted for a particular Z Benefit cancer package. Check the specific list for what you need.

## Cancer Screening Providers: A Different List

If you want screening rather than treatment, PhilHealth has a separate **"List of Accredited Providers for Selected Outpatient Cancer Screening Tests for CY 2026,"** updated as of September 30, 2026. It runs 20 pages and lists 415 facilities, spread from Cordillera to BARMM. This includes government hospitals, private hospitals, and some district hospitals and health units.

The list shows which tests each facility is accredited for. The test columns are:

- Mammogram
- Breast ultrasound
- Low-dose chest CT scan
- Liver ultrasound
- Alpha-fetoprotein (AFP) blood test
- Colonoscopy

Not every facility offers every test. Check the tick marks in the official PDF for the test you need.

### How screening is accessed

Under PhilHealth Circular No. 2026-0007, which sets standards for the YAKAP program, YAKAP Clinics are expected to coordinate referrals to cancer screening providers. PhilHealth's quality standards circulars for breast, colorectal, liver, and lung cancer screening (Circulars 2025-0024 to 2025-0027, referencing Circular 2025-0014) say screening is for **eligible, asymptomatic** people and is offered by PhilHealth-accredited primary care physicians after an eligibility assessment.

In plain terms: screening is generally a **preventive** service you reach through your registered YAKAP Clinic, not a substitute for seeing a doctor about symptoms. If you already have symptoms or a diagnosis, talk to a doctor about the right next step.

## Z Benefit Cancer Packages: Check the Contracted List

Z Benefits are PhilHealth's packages for certain high-cost illnesses. Cancer packages in the official contracted list are for ALL, breast, prostate, cervical, colon, and rectal cancer.

What PhilHealth's official materials say that is useful to know:

- **Breast cancer:** PhilHealth's Z Benefit package covers stages 0 to 4 under Circular No. 2024-0007, with a maximum of ₱1.4 million as stated in PhilHealth's own news release (May 2025).
- **Pre-authorization:** Z Benefit care involves a pre-authorization request submitted by the contracted facility to PhilHealth. PhilHealth has an official Pre-authorization Checklist and Request Form for breast cancer and updated it through Advisory No. 2024-0021.
- **Facility must be contracted:** Only facilities on the contracted Z Benefit list for that specific cancer are covered under that package. A hospital on the general accredited hospital list is not automatically a Z Benefit provider.
- **Amounts for other cancers:** The official pages I reviewed for this article did not give a single current amount for each of the other Z cancer packages in one place, so they are not listed here. Ask the contracted facility's Z Benefit coordinator or check PhilHealth's Benefits page.

Official list: [Contracted Health Facilities (updated Sept. 30, 2026)](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/partners/providers/facilities/contracted/).

## Using PhilHealth in an Accredited Hospital for Cancer Admission

For regular hospital admissions, PhilHealth benefits are claimed through the accredited hospital and applied to the bill under the applicable case rate rules. **PhilHealth coverage depends on the applicable benefit package and eligibility requirements.** It does not mean the entire bill is paid. You may still have costs not covered by the case rate or package.

Before admission, ask the hospital's PhilHealth or billing desk:

1. Is the hospital accredited as of today?
2. Which benefit applies to this treatment (regular case rate, Z Benefit, or outpatient)?
3. What documents do I need to bring?
4. What costs would not be covered?

## Step-by-Step: How to Check a Facility Before You Go

1. **Decide what you need.** Treatment center, hospital admission, screening test, or Z Benefit care? Each uses a different list.
2. **Open PhilHealth's Accredited Health Facilities page.** Note the "Updated as of" date at the top.
3. **Open the matching PDF.** Use Cancer Treatment Facilities, Hospitals and Infirmaries/Dispensaries, or Cancer Screening Test Providers.
4. **Search the PDF.** Use Ctrl+F (or the find option on your phone) with the facility name and city.
5. **Check the listed expiry date** and the services (for screening, the ticked tests).
6. **For Z Benefit cancers,** also search the Z Benefit contracted list for that specific cancer.
7. **Call or message the facility** using the contact details in the official PDF to confirm services, current status, and what to bring.
8. **If anything is unclear,** contact PhilHealth through the Action Center listed on its website.

## Common Problems

- **"My hospital isn't on the cancer treatment list."** That list covers standalone cancer treatment facilities. The hospital may still be accredited on the Hospitals list or the Cancer Screening list. Check each.
- **"The list I found online is old."** PhilHealth updates its lists; the version on the official site is the one to trust. Look for the "Updated as of" date.
- **"Accreditation expires 12/31/2026."** This is a listed expiry date on the current list. Ask the facility or PhilHealth whether renewal for the next cycle is in process.
- **"Screening is not available near me."** Check the Cancer Screening list by province and city; many provincial and district hospitals are included. Different facilities offer different tests.
- **"I was told PhilHealth will cover everything."** Ask for written details of what the package covers and what it does not.

## What to Do If Something Goes Wrong

- If a facility says it is accredited but you cannot find it on the official list, ask it for its PhilHealth accreditation details and verify with PhilHealth.
- If your claim is denied or the benefit is not applied, ask the hospital's PhilHealth desk for the reason in writing and contact PhilHealth through its Action Center.
- Keep copies of your PhilHealth ID/MDR, valid IDs, hospital documents, and any receipts.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is there one official list of PhilHealth accredited cancer hospitals?**
No. PhilHealth publishes several lists: Cancer Treatment Facilities, Hospitals, Cancer Screening Test Providers, and Z Benefit contracted providers. Which one you need depends on the service.

**How current is the PhilHealth list?**
At the time of this review (October 26, 2026), PhilHealth's accredited and contracted facility pages showed "updated as of September 30, 2026."

**What is the difference between accredited and Z Benefit contracted?**
Accredited means recognized by PhilHealth to serve members. Contracted for Z Benefit means the facility has a contract for a specific Z package, such as breast cancer.

**Can I get cancer screening under PhilHealth?**
PhilHealth has benefit packages for selected outpatient screening tests for breast, lung, liver, and colorectal cancer, accessed through YAKAP. Eligibility is assessed by an accredited primary care physician.

**Does PhilHealth pay the whole cancer treatment bill?**
Not necessarily. Coverage depends on the benefit package, case rate, and eligibility. The breast cancer Z Benefit has a stated maximum of ₱1.4 million; costs beyond a package can still apply.

**Are there accredited cancer treatment facilities in Mindanao?**
The September 30, 2026 Cancer Treatment Facilities list shows no Mindanao entries. Mindanao hospitals may still appear on the Hospitals list or Cancer Screening list.

**Who do I contact with questions?**
PhilHealth Regional Offices or its Action Center. PhilHealth's advisory lists actioncenter@philhealth.gov.ph as an email contact.
