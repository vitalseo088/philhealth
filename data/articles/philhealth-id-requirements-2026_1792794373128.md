# 2. Article

# PhilHealth ID Requirements 2026: How to Get or Replace Your PhilHealth ID

For a first PhilHealth ID, PhilHealth's published process is to submit a filled-out **PhilHealth Member Registration Form (PMRF)** with a **valid proof of identity** at a PhilHealth office. The ID card and your **Member Data Record (MDR)** are then issued to you. PhilHealth has said many times that the ID is free.

This guide is for members who are registering for the first time, or who lost their card or are not sure what they need. It explains what PhilHealth's official pages say, and it is open about what they do not say. For example, PhilHealth's published pages that we reviewed do not list one official set of accepted IDs, or a step-by-step process for replacing a lost card.

## Quick Answer

| Situation | What PhilHealth's sources say |
|---|---|
| New member, first PhilHealth ID | PMRF plus valid proof of identity, submitted at a Local Health Insurance Office (LHIO) or PhilHealth Express outlet |
| Lost or damaged card | PhilHealth's pages we reviewed do not give a dedicated procedure. Ask the nearest office; see the section on replacement below |
| Cost | PhilHealth says it does not charge fees for issuing PhilHealth IDs to legitimate members |
| Your PIN | Unique and permanent. A lost card does not change it |
| Need proof of membership quickly | You can print your MDR from the Member Portal |

## What Is a PhilHealth ID?

The **PhilHealth ID card** (PhilHealth Identification Card) shows your **PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN)**. PhilHealth's registration form says the PIN is your unique and permanent number and should be used in all transactions with PhilHealth. Your membership is tied to the PIN, not to the card.

Do not confuse the card with these related items:

- **PMRF:** the form you submit to register or update your records.
- **MDR:** PhilHealth's record of your membership and declared dependents, which you can view and print through the Member Portal.

## Requirements for a New PhilHealth ID

### Self-earning and informal economy members

PhilHealth's page for informal economy members gives this process:

1. Visit any Local Health Insurance Office (LHIO) or PhilHealth Express outlet.
2. Fill out **two copies** of the PMRF, which you can download from PhilHealth's Downloads page.
3. Submit the PMRF at the office.
4. Wait for your **MDR and PhilHealth ID card**.
5. Pay your premium using your PIN.

### Employees

PhilHealth's page for formal economy members says to submit a duly accomplished PMRF. An older PhilHealth advisory on issuing IDs and MDRs to employed members (2011) said the PMRF should come with a clear copy of the employee's birth or baptismal certificate or one valid ID, and a clear copy of the legal supporting document for any declared dependent. That advisory is old, so confirm with your employer or PhilHealth office.

### What the PMRF itself says about identity documents

The instructions printed on the PMRF say a properly accomplished form should be accompanied by a **valid proof of identity for first-time registrants**. Names should follow the birth certificate format. The form also asks for a mobile number as a required field.

### Which IDs count as valid proof of identity?

The official pages we reviewed do **not** list one current set of accepted IDs. Bring an original government-issued ID with your photo and signature, and ask the office whether it will accept it. Do not assume that a particular ID, such as a company ID, will be accepted.

## Replacing a Lost or Damaged PhilHealth ID

PhilHealth's own pages that we could open do not describe a replacement procedure. What we found:

- A secondary summary of PhilHealth's 2026 Citizen's Charter reports a PhilHealth Express service titled "Request for Copy of PhilHealth Identification Cards and Member Data Records," with a request form, a valid ID with photo and signature, a photocopy of that ID, and the PIN if known. The same summary reports no fee and no affidavit of loss for that service. We could not open the Citizen's Charter itself, so treat this as unconfirmed.
- Other websites say an affidavit of loss or a replacement fee of about ₱90 is required. We could not find these in PhilHealth's own pages, and they conflict with PhilHealth's statement that it does not charge for ID issuance.

Because these sources disagree, call or visit the nearest PhilHealth office before you go and ask exactly what to bring. If someone else will pick up the card for you, ask what authorization they need.

## Is the PhilHealth ID Free?

PhilHealth says yes. In Advisory No. 2017-0034, PhilHealth said it does not charge any fees for the issuance of PhilHealth ID cards to legitimate members, and asked members to transact only at their nearest LHIO or PhilHealth Express. A 2021 PhilHealth announcement said PhilHealth IDs and MDRs are free, and that members can print their MDRs through the Member Portal without paying.

Report anyone who asks for payment to the PhilHealth Action Center at **(02) 8662-2588** or **actioncenter@philhealth.gov.ph**. Contact details can change, so confirm them on philhealth.gov.ph.

## Do You Need the Physical ID to Use PhilHealth?

PhilHealth's sources we reviewed do not state clearly whether a hospital can require the card. What is documented:

- PhilHealth's 2026 circular on leptospirosis cites the Universal Health Care Act, which says every Filipino citizen is automatically included in the National Health Insurance Program, and says claims submission for that benefit does not need a printed MDR.
- Since Circular No. 02-2014, hospitals can generate an eligibility form through PhilHealth's provider portal, which the circular says replaces or is an alternative to the MDR as proof of membership.

So your PIN and records matter most. If you lose the card, you can still look up your PIN on your MDR, which you can print from the Member Portal, but ask the hospital what it will accept.

## Can I Use My PhilHealth ID as a Valid ID Elsewhere?

The PhilHealth sources we reviewed do not say which agencies or businesses accept the PhilHealth ID as proof of identity. Each organization sets its own list. Ask the organization before relying on it.

## Step-by-Step: What To Do

1. **Check whether you already have a PIN.** Look at an old MDR, payslip or PhilHealth correspondence, or contact the Action Center.
2. **Download the PMRF** from the Downloads page on philhealth.gov.ph.
3. **Fill it out** in capital letters, with your name as shown on your birth certificate. Mark it for registration, or for updating if your records need correcting.
4. **Prepare a valid proof of identity.** Ask the office which IDs it accepts.
5. **Visit an LHIO or PhilHealth Express outlet.** PhilHealth's Directory of Offices lists locations and, for some outlets, the days and hours they open. Check before going.
6. **Keep a copy** of everything you submit and of your MDR.

## Common Problems

- **You paid someone to get your ID.** PhilHealth says ID issuance is free and warns against fixers. Do not pay intermediaries.
- **The name on your ID does not match your records.** Submit a PMRF marked for updating. The sources we reviewed do not list the documents for each correction, so ask the office.
- **Your PIN is not on a card you no longer have.** Check your MDR.
- **An office asks for something you did not expect.** Ask them to tell you which PhilHealth rule or advisory requires it, and contact the Action Center if you are unsure.

## What To Do If Something Goes Wrong

- Contact the PhilHealth Action Center at **(02) 8662-2588** or **actioncenter@philhealth.gov.ph**, or visit your nearest Regional Office or LHIO.
- Keep your PMRF copy and any receipt or acknowledgment you are given.
- Use only philhealth.gov.ph. PhilHealth has warned members to check that the website address ends in gov.ph and not .com or .net.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What do I need to get a PhilHealth ID?
A filled-out PMRF and a valid proof of identity, submitted at a PhilHealth office. For informal economy members, PhilHealth says to submit two copies of the PMRF.

### Is the PhilHealth ID free?
PhilHealth says it does not charge fees for issuing PhilHealth IDs to legitimate members.

### Is an affidavit of loss required to replace a lost ID?
The PhilHealth pages we reviewed do not say. Secondary websites disagree. Ask the office.

### Do I need 1x1 photos?
The PMRF and PhilHealth pages we reviewed do not mention photos. Some websites do. Ask the office.

### Can I apply for the PhilHealth ID online?
PhilHealth's Member Portal offers MDR printing and record updates. The PhilHealth pages we reviewed do not describe online issuance of the physical card, and we could not verify claims on other websites about a digital PhilHealth ID.

### What is the difference between the PhilHealth ID and the MDR?
The ID card shows your PIN. The MDR is the record of your membership and declared dependents.

### Does losing my card cancel my membership?
PhilHealth says the PIN is permanent. We found no PhilHealth source saying that losing the card affects membership.

### Where can I find the nearest office?
PhilHealth's Directory of Offices on philhealth.gov.ph lists Regional Offices, LHIOs and PhilHealth Express outlets.
