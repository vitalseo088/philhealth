# PhilHealth MDR Requirements 2026: How to Get, Print and Update Your Member Data Record

To print your PhilHealth **Member Data Record (MDR)** online, you need your **PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN)** and an account on PhilHealth's Member Portal. To correct or update what is on it, you submit a **PhilHealth Member Registration Form (PMRF)** marked for updating, with supporting documents where needed.

This guide is for members and dependents who need a copy of their MDR, want to fix an error on it, or are not sure whether a hospital will ask for it. The details below come from PhilHealth's own pages, advisories and circulars. Where PhilHealth's published sources do not say something clearly, this guide says so instead of guessing.

## Quick Answer

| What you want to do | What PhilHealth's sources say you need |
|---|---|
| Print or view your current MDR | A Member Portal account, created with your PIN and activated through your email |
| Get your first MDR (new member) | A PMRF marked for registration and a valid proof of identity, submitted at a PhilHealth office |
| Correct or update your MDR | A PMRF marked for updating or amendment, plus supporting documents |
| Add or fix a dependent | A PMRF with the dependent declared, plus documents that establish the relationship to the member |
| Show a hospital your MDR | Check with the hospital. PhilHealth's recent circulars do not make a printed MDR mandatory for claims under at least one current benefit package |

## What Is a PhilHealth MDR?

The **Member Data Record (MDR)** is PhilHealth's record of your membership. PhilHealth describes it as showing your membership profile, such as name, date of birth, address, employer and the names of your declared dependents. It is generated by PhilHealth after your registration is processed. It is not a form you fill out.

Do not confuse these three items:

- **PIN:** your 12-digit PhilHealth Identification Number. PhilHealth says it is unique and permanent, and should be used in all transactions with PhilHealth.
- **PMRF:** the form you submit to register or to update your information.
- **MDR:** the record PhilHealth produces from that registration, which you can view and print.

## How to Print Your MDR Online

PhilHealth's Online Services page lists the Member Portal as the place to access your records and contributions, and to view or print your MDR. A 2021 PhilHealth announcement said members can download and print their MDRs through the portal without travel or payment.

1. Go to **philhealth.gov.ph** and open **Online Services**, then the **Member Portal**.
2. If you do not have an account, register using your PIN and confirm the activation message sent to your email address.
3. Log in and open your member information.
4. Choose the option to print or view your MDR, then save or print it.

Use only the official PhilHealth website, whose address ends in **gov.ph**. PhilHealth has reminded members to check the domain and avoid look-alike sites ending in .com or .net.

PhilHealth's published sources do not describe a standard process for recovering a forgotten PIN. If you cannot find yours, contact the PhilHealth Action Center (details below).

## Requirements by Situation

### First-time registration

PhilHealth's page for informal economy members says to visit a Local Health Insurance Office (LHIO) or PhilHealth Express outlet, fill out two copies of the PMRF, submit it, and wait for your MDR and PhilHealth ID card. The instructions printed on the PMRF say a properly filled-out form must be accompanied by a **valid proof of identity** for first-time registrants.

Employees are registered through the same form. PhilHealth's page for formal economy members says to submit a duly accomplished PMRF.

### Updating or correcting your MDR

PhilHealth's amendment pages give these steps for members:

1. Download the PMRF from PhilHealth's Downloads page.
2. Tick **For Updating** in the upper right corner.
3. Fill out the form, indicating the correct information.
4. Submit the PMRF to the nearest PhilHealth office.
5. Wait for the printout of your updated MDR.

The PMRF lists the kinds of changes it covers: change or correction of name, correction of date of birth, correction of sex, change of civil status, and updating of personal information such as address, telephone number, mobile number and email address.

**Supporting documents:** the PMRF instructions say updating or amendment requests should be accompanied by supporting documents, and specifically mention documents that establish the relationship between the member and a dependent. The pages we reviewed do **not** list a specific document for each type of correction, such as a name or birth date change. Ask the PhilHealth office which documents it will accept for your correction before you go.

PhilHealth's 2026 advisory on the Member Portal says members may also update or amend their membership record through the portal, and are advised to keep details such as income, contact number, address and email address accurate. A 2021 announcement described another option: sending a scanned PMRF with supporting documents by email to an address provided by the regional office. Check with your regional office whether that option is currently available.

### Declaring dependents

PhilHealth states that qualified dependents must be declared by the principal member, and that their names must be listed under the member's MDR for hassle-free benefit availment. The qualified dependents listed on PhilHealth's page are:

| Dependent | Condition listed by PhilHealth |
|---|---|
| Legitimate spouse | Not a member |
| Children (legitimate, legitimated, acknowledged, illegitimate as shown on the birth certificate, adopted or stepchildren) | Below 21 years old, unmarried and unemployed |
| Children 21 or older | With a congenital or acquired disability that makes them totally dependent on the member, as determined by PhilHealth |
| Foster child | As defined in the Foster Care Act of 2012 |
| Parents | 60 or older, not an enrolled member, with monthly income below an amount PhilHealth determines |
| Parents with permanent disability | Totally dependent on the member, regardless of age |

The PMRF also says dependents with disability should be registered as principal members under the law on mandatory PhilHealth coverage for persons with disability.

## Do Hospitals Still Require a Printed MDR?

PhilHealth's published sources do not give one simple answer, because the rules changed over the years.

- Older PhilHealth advisories called an updated MDR the basic document for benefit availment.
- Under PhilHealth Circular No. 02-2014, hospitals began generating a **PhilHealth Benefit Eligibility Form (PBEF)** through the provider portal, which the circular says replaces or is an alternative to the MDR as proof of membership or dependency.
- In 2023, during a PhilHealth system disruption, a PhilHealth advisory asked members to submit a photocopy of their MDR or PhilHealth ID to the healthcare facility. That was an advisory for that period.
- PhilHealth's 2026 circular on the leptospirosis benefit says claims submission does not require a printed copy of the MDR. That statement is for that benefit package.

What this means in practice is not spelled out for every hospital. A printed MDR may still be useful to show your PIN and declared dependents, and it costs nothing to print from the portal, so many members bring one. Ask the hospital's PhilHealth desk what it will accept.

## Examples

*These are illustrations only.*

- **Example 1:** A member gets married and wants to add a spouse. The member submits a PMRF marked for updating with the spouse declared, along with documents that show the relationship, then reprints the MDR to check that the spouse appears.
- **Example 2:** A member sees a misspelled surname on the printed MDR. The member submits a PMRF marked for correction of name and asks the PhilHealth office which supporting documents it needs.

## Common Problems

- **You cannot print your MDR online.** You need both a PIN and a Member Portal account activated through email. A PIN alone is not an account.
- **A dependent does not appear.** PhilHealth says dependents must be declared by the principal member and listed under the MDR. Submit a PMRF update with supporting documents.
- **The record still shows old information after an update.** Reprint the MDR after the update is processed. If it is still wrong, contact PhilHealth with a copy of what you submitted.
- **Not sure which form version to use.** PhilHealth's Downloads page lists the PMRF as of its last check. Download it from the official page rather than using a saved copy.

## What To Do If Something Goes Wrong

- Keep a copy of your submitted PMRF, your supporting documents and any acknowledgment.
- Contact the PhilHealth Action Center at **(02) 8662-2588** or **actioncenter@philhealth.gov.ph**, the contact details in PhilHealth's 2026 advisory. You can also visit your nearest Regional Office or LHIO. Contact details can change, so check philhealth.gov.ph.
- If you are in a hospital and the PhilHealth desk says your record is incomplete, ask what exactly is missing and request it in writing if possible.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between the MDR and the PMRF?
The PMRF is the form you submit to register or update your information. The MDR is the record PhilHealth generates from your registration.

### How do I get a copy of my MDR?
Log in to the PhilHealth Member Portal with your PIN-based account and print it. If you do not have an account, register with your PIN and activate it through your email.

### Is there a fee for printing my MDR?
PhilHealth's 2021 announcement described printing the MDR through the portal as free of travel and payment. The pages we reviewed do not list any fee for the printout at an office either, but confirm with the office.

### What do I need to update my MDR?
A PMRF marked for updating, with the correct information, and supporting documents. The sources we reviewed do not list the exact documents for each type of change, so ask the PhilHealth office.

### Can my dependents have their own MDR?
PhilHealth says dependents are listed under the principal member's MDR. Dependents with disability are to be registered as principal members under the law on mandatory PhilHealth coverage for persons with disability.

### Do I need a PhilHealth ID card?
PhilHealth's page for informal economy members says members wait for an MDR and a PhilHealth ID card after registration. The sources we reviewed do not clearly state whether the card is needed for hospital admission.

### Where do I download the PMRF?
From the Downloads page on philhealth.gov.ph.
