# YAKAP Clinic Appointment 2026: How to Schedule Your First Visit

To get a YAKAP clinic appointment, you first have to **select a YAKAP clinic** (through the eGov PH Super App, the PhilHealth Member Portal, a PhilHealth office, or other official channels). The clinic then schedules your **First Patient Encounter (FPE)**, your first visit of the year. PhilHealth's rules do not describe a central, nationwide booking system. In practice, the appointment comes from your chosen clinic.

This guide is for PhilHealth members and dependents who have picked, or are about to pick, a YAKAP clinic and want to know what happens next. It reflects PhilHealth issuances available as of **October 2026**. It does not cover benefit amounts or the medicine list, and it is not medical advice.

## Quick Answer

| Question | Short answer |
|---|---|
| Who sets my appointment? | Your chosen YAKAP clinic. PhilHealth's circular says the clinic notifies you of your FPE schedule. |
| What is the first appointment called? | **First Patient Encounter (FPE)**. It is not a medical consultation. |
| Do I have to select a clinic first? | Yes. The clinic checks daily for members who selected it, then schedules their FPEs. |
| How will I be notified? | PhilHealth or the clinic notifies you. For online selection, the circular says notifications go by push notification, email and/or SMS, depending on the platform. |
| What do I sign at the visit? | The **YAKAP Empanelment Slip (YES)**, also called the Mutual Care Agreement. |
| Will I pay? | Public YAKAP clinics should not charge for covered services. Private clinics may charge a co-payment capped at ₱900 per member per year. |

## What Is a YAKAP Clinic Appointment?

YAKAP stands for *Yaman ng Kalusugan Program*, PhilHealth's primary care benefit. Your "appointment" usually means one of two things:

1. **Your First Patient Encounter (FPE).** PhilHealth describes this as the first patient contact for the year, where the clinic takes or updates your basic health data to identify your health risks. PhilHealth states that the **FPE is not a medical consultation**.
2. **Later consultations and follow-ups** at the same clinic, once you are empaneled. *Empanelment* means the formal care relationship between you and your clinic, created after your FPE.

## Before You Can Get an Appointment

You need a few things in place first:

1. **A PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN).** If you do not have one, PhilHealth's circular says to register online or through assisted registration at a Local Health Insurance Office (LHIO), the Corporate Action Center or P-CARES.
2. **A selected, accredited YAKAP clinic.** Empanelment to an unaccredited primary care facility is not valid YAKAP registration, according to Advisory No. 2026-0023.
3. **A fixed, permanent clinic.** Mobile units and telemedicine-only providers cannot be your YAKAP clinic.

PhilHealth's standard is a clinic within 30 km of your declared home or office address. A farther clinic is allowed if it is your informed choice, but PhilHealth strongly discourages it.

Minors cannot transact on their own. A parent or legal guardian handles clinic selection and signs the agreement for them. Adults aged 18 and above can do it themselves.

## How to Get Your YAKAP Appointment: Step by Step

### Step 1: Select your YAKAP clinic

Use any official channel from PhilHealth's circular:

- **eGov PH Super App:** open the app, go to NGAs, search for PhilHealth, select PhilHealth Member Portal, open the YAKAP tab, then choose *Add YAKAP Clinic* for yourself or a dependent.
- **PhilHealth Member Portal:** log in with your PIN, select *Click here to Select YAKAP Clinic*, search by province or city/municipality, and pick a clinic. For dependents, use the YAKAP Clinic column beside each dependent's date of birth.
- **Any PhilHealth office (LHIO):** you can use a dedicated terminal or ask for assisted selection.
- **The YAKAP clinic:** the clinic can help you register and select using the app or portal.
- **Other channels:** the PhilHealth Corporate Action Center, or P-CARES staff in hospitals.

### Step 2: Wait for confirmation and your FPE schedule

After you select a clinic, PhilHealth processes and confirms your selection and notifies the clinic. Then the clinic or PhilHealth notifies you of the successful selection, and the clinic notifies you of your FPE schedule. PhilHealth also tells clinics to check daily for members who selected them and to schedule their FPEs.

PhilHealth's documents do not state how many days this takes. We do not give a timeframe.

### Step 3: Confirm your appointment with the clinic

If you have not heard from the clinic, contact it directly. The clinic's exact booking method (phone, walk-in, online) is not specified in the PhilHealth documents we reviewed. Ask the clinic:

- What date and time is my FPE?
- What should I bring?
- Is the clinic public or private, and is there a co-payment? (Private clinics must tell members about their co-payment structure at empanelment, at the FPE and at every subsequent visit.)
- What are the clinic hours?

### Step 4: Attend your First Patient Encounter

At the clinic, expect the following, based on PhilHealth's circular and advisories:

- **Identity check.** The clinic verifies your identity through the **PhilHealth Check Utility (PCU)**, an online tool that does a selfie-based liveness check against PhilSys and PhilHealth records. This is done before the FPE and before each consultation.
- **Basic health data collection.** The clinic takes or updates your basic health information to identify your risks.
- **Signing the YAKAP Empanelment Slip (YES).** After the FPE, you and the clinic confirm empanelment. PhilHealth Advisory No. 2026-0029 says the digital YES is the primary and required platform for empanelment.

### Step 5: Return for consultations as needed

Once you complete your FPE and sign the YES, you have the right to continuous and appropriate primary care from your empaneled clinic, according to Advisory No. 2026-0003.

## What to Bring to Your Appointment

| Item | Status |
|---|---|
| Your **PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN)** | Needed. Clinic selection depends on it. |
| A **valid government-issued ID** | Required if you are **not PhilSys-registered**, because the PCU only processes PhilSys-registered individuals. For others, bringing one is a sensible precaution. |
| A phone or device for the **liveness check** | The circular says the PCU can be done on your own phone or on a clinic terminal. |
| Anything else | The official documents we reviewed do not list other documents. Ask your clinic. |

## Cost: Will I Pay for My YAKAP Appointment?

PhilHealth Advisory No. 2026-0003 (January 8, 2026) sets out your rights:

| Clinic type | What PhilHealth says |
|---|---|
| **Public YAKAP clinic** | You should not be charged any fee. Covered consultations, basic laboratory services and other included primary care services must be free. |
| **Private YAKAP clinic** | Your out-of-pocket co-payment is capped at **₱900 per member per year**. After you reach the cap, remaining covered and necessary YAKAP services for the year should be provided at no additional cost. |

Private clinics may structure the co-payment in different ways. PhilHealth's co-payment guidelines (Advisory No. 2025-0057) say private providers must tell members about their co-payment structure and clearly explain the difference between the co-payment and capitation (the fixed payment PhilHealth makes to the clinic).

## Example (Illustrative Only)

*This is an example, not a real case.* Ramon selects a clinic through the Member Portal. A few days later he has not received a message, so he calls the clinic to ask about his FPE schedule. The staff give him a date, ask him to bring his PIN and a valid ID, and tell him the clinic is private with a co-payment structure they will explain at the visit. At the FPE, staff verify his identity through the PCU, record his basic health data, and he signs the YES.

## Common Problems

- **The clinic has not contacted you.** Call the clinic first. If the problem continues, visit an LHIO or contact the Corporate Action Center.
- **You did not attend your FPE.** PhilHealth says members who selected a clinic but did not complete an FPE are not retained for the next calendar year. You can choose the same clinic again or a different one.
- **The clinic refuses you.** PhilHealth's circular says a clinic cannot reject a member who selected it, unless it has reached its declared beneficiary limit.
- **You are asked to pay for covered services in a public clinic.** Under Advisory 2026-0003, this should not happen.
- **You are denied services after FPE and YES.** The same advisory says you should not be denied primary care services.
- **Identity check problems.** If you are not PhilSys-registered, bring a valid ID for manual validation.
- **You want a different clinic.** See the transfer rules below.

## Changing Clinics If You Cannot Get an Appointment

| Your situation | Rule (PhilHealth Circular No. 2025-0017) |
|---|---|
| Selected a clinic but **have not had your FPE** | You can transfer at any time of the year by visiting your nearest LHIO. It takes effect right after processing. |
| **Already empaneled** | Transfers are allowed in the **fourth quarter**, effective January of the next year. |
| Empaneled, but the clinic closed, lost accreditation or got a DOH cease-and-desist order | You may transfer earlier. |

## What To Do If Something Goes Wrong

PhilHealth Advisory No. 2026-0003 invites members to raise concerns with the 24/7 Corporate Action Center if they are asked to pay fees that should be covered, or are denied services despite being empaneled:

- **Call:** (02) 866-225-88
- **Text:** 0917-127-5987
- **Email:** yakaptugon@philhealth.gov.ph

Keep your PIN, a screenshot of your clinic selection and your signed YES.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I just walk in to a YAKAP clinic?
The PhilHealth documents we reviewed do not say whether a clinic must accept walk-ins or require booking. Call the clinic first. PhilHealth's circular does say clinics may help you select them on-site using the app or portal.

### How long does it take to get my FPE schedule?
PhilHealth's official documents do not give a processing time, so we do not state one.

### Is the First Patient Encounter a check-up?
No. PhilHealth states the FPE is not a medical consultation. It is the clinic's first contact to take or update your basic health data and identify health risks.

### Do I have to pay for the First Patient Encounter?
Under Advisory 2026-0003, public clinics should not charge for covered services, and private clinics may collect a co-payment capped at ₱900 per member per year. Ask a private clinic how it structures it.

### Do my dependents need separate appointments?
Dependents select a clinic separately in the Member Portal. Each beneficiary completes their own FPE.

### Can I book an appointment online through PhilHealth?
PhilHealth's online platforms are described as selection channels. The circular says platforms facilitate FPE scheduling through notifications. We did not find an official online appointment-booking tool.

### Do I need to repeat the FPE every year?
The circular defines the FPE as the first patient contact "for the year," and members retained at a clinic sign a new YES annually. Ask your clinic what it requires for the new year.

### What if I selected a clinic but never went?
You will not be retained for the next calendar year and would need to select a clinic again.

### How do I find a YAKAP clinic near me?
Use the eGov PH Super App or the Member Portal, which let you search by province and city or municipality.
