# PhilHealth YAKAP for Senior Citizens: How to Register and What You Can Get (2026)

If you are 60 or older, you can use PhilHealth YAKAP once you have a PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN), have chosen a YAKAP Clinic, and have completed a First Patient Encounter at that clinic. YAKAP (Yaman ng Kalusugan Program) is PhilHealth's outpatient primary care benefit. It brings together check-ups, laboratory tests, referral for select cancer screening, and outpatient medicines through a benefit called GAMOT.

This guide is for seniors, their children, and caregivers. It reflects PhilHealth issuances available as of October 24, 2026. YAKAP is outpatient care, so it is separate from the hospital (inpatient) benefits PhilHealth pays to accredited hospitals.

## Quick Answer

| Question | Short answer |
|---|---|
| Who can use YAKAP? | PhilHealth's YAKAP selection rules apply to all Filipinos, whether a member or a qualified dependent. |
| Are seniors automatically covered by PhilHealth? | Seniors 60 and above are covered by law, but you still need a PIN, which means registering with PhilHealth. |
| Do seniors pay premiums? | Seniors enrolled under the senior citizen category are funded through the Sin Tax Law. Seniors who are still working or have a regular income continue paying under their applicable category. |
| What do I need first? | A PhilHealth PIN. |
| How do I choose a clinic? | eGovPH app, PhilHealth Member Portal, any PhilHealth office, or with help from a YAKAP Clinic. |
| What does it cost? | Nothing in public YAKAP clinics. In private clinics, your co-payment is capped at ₱900 per member per year. |
| What about medicines? | GAMOT has an annual benefit limit of ₱20,000 per beneficiary, used with a prescription from a YAKAP physician. |

## What Is PhilHealth YAKAP?

YAKAP is the evolution of the older PhilHealth Konsulta program. It is built to keep members healthier through regular primary care instead of waiting until a hospital stay is needed. PhilHealth's 2026 circular describes it as integrating three things:

- primary care services (consultations, risk assessment, follow-ups),
- the PhilHealth GAMOT outpatient medicines benefit, and
- select cancer screening tests.

You may still see the word "Konsulta" on older forms or in older articles. The current program name is YAKAP.

As of May 15, 2026, PhilHealth reported 4,287 accredited YAKAP Clinics nationwide, reaching 33.2 million Filipinos.

## Who Is Eligible? (Senior Citizen Rules)

PhilHealth's senior citizen page lists who qualifies for senior citizen membership:

- Filipino citizens who live in the Philippines, are 60 or older, and are not covered by any other PhilHealth membership category.
- Qualified dependents of senior citizen members who are also seniors themselves (for example, a spouse who is also 60 or older).
- Seniors who are qualified dependents of members in other categories.

Two points that often cause confusion:

- **"Automatic" still needs registration.** Coverage by law does not give you a PIN by itself. To use YAKAP, you must be registered and have a PIN.
- **Seniors with income may still pay.** Seniors who are gainfully employed or have regular sources of income continue to pay premiums under the membership category that applies to them.

## What You Need to Register

For senior citizen membership with PhilHealth:

| Item | Where it applies |
|---|---|
| PhilHealth Member Registration Form (PMRF) | Required for both registration routes below |
| One 1x1 photo taken within the last six months | When registering at a PhilHealth Local Health Insurance Office (LHIO) |
| Senior Citizen ID issued by your city or municipal Office for Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA), or a valid government-issued ID | When registering at an LHIO |

Not every senior has a PhilSys (national ID) record. For assisted YAKAP clinic selection, PhilHealth's identity check tool only processes PhilSys-registered people. If you are not PhilSys-registered, you must present a valid government-issued ID or alternative documents for manual validation.

## Step-by-Step: How to Use YAKAP as a Senior Citizen

The four steps in PhilHealth's rules are: registration, selection, First Patient Encounter, and empanelment.

### Step 1: Get your PhilHealth PIN

You have two options if you are not yet a PhilHealth member:

1. **Through your OSCA:** Fill out one PMRF, submit it to the OSCA in the city or municipality where you live, then wait for your Member Data Record (MDR) and PhilHealth ID card issued through the OSCA.
2. **Through a PhilHealth LHIO:** Fill out the PMRF, attach the 1x1 photo, present your Senior Citizen ID or a valid government ID, and wait for your MDR and PhilHealth ID card.

Already have a PIN (for example, you were a member before)? Skip to Step 2. If your records are out of date, update them first, because clinics and PhilHealth check your identity.

### Step 2: Choose a YAKAP Clinic

You can choose a clinic through any of these channels:

- **eGovPH app:** Go to PhilHealth services, open the YAKAP tab, and choose "Add YAKAP Clinic" (it also works for dependents).
- **PhilHealth Member Portal:** Log in with your PIN and select "Click here to Select YAKAP Clinic," then search by province or city/municipality.
- **Any PhilHealth office:** Staff can help through a terminal, or through assisted selection where you verify your identity and submit a Primary Care Selection Form.
- **At a YAKAP Clinic:** The clinic can help you register and choose it using the eGovPH app or the Member Portal.

Other channels exist too. You may call the PhilHealth Corporate Action Center and send a completed selection form plus a government ID, or get help from PhilHealth staff stationed in hospitals.

Before you choose, check the clinic is accredited. PhilHealth publishes its list of accredited YAKAP Clinics on its website. Provider information can change, so check the official PhilHealth provider directory before visiting.

### Step 3: Complete your First Patient Encounter (FPE)

After you select a clinic, PhilHealth notifies the clinic and the clinic must check for new selections daily and schedule your FPE. The FPE is the first contact of the year where the clinic takes or updates your basic health data to identify health risks. PhilHealth states it is **not yet a consultation**.

### Step 4: Sign your YAKAP Empanelment Slip

After your FPE you and the clinic sign the Mutual Care Agreement (also called the YAKAP Empanelment Slip). This makes you "empaneled": the clinic becomes your regular primary care provider for the rest of the calendar year, until December 31. You sign a new one each year to continue.

Bring a valid ID to every visit. Clinics verify identity before consultations.

## What YAKAP Gives You

PhilHealth describes YAKAP as covering free medical consultations, essential laboratory and diagnostic services, medicines under GAMOT, and outpatient cancer screening. PhilHealth coverage depends on the applicable benefit package and eligibility requirements, and this is not medical advice. Your doctor decides what tests or medicines you need.

At minimum, YAKAP Clinics must provide:

- a First Patient Encounter and empanelment,
- initial assessment and risk profiling,
- primary care consultations, laboratory tests and medicines,
- referral to higher-level facilities and other services, such as cancer screening, and
- keeping your health records.

Your YAKAP Clinic also acts as your "navigator": other facilities (such as screening centers and pharmacies) are expected to refer you back to your clinic.

### Cancer screening

Your YAKAP Clinic refers you for screening based on your health risk profile. PhilHealth's circular points to its quality standards for breast, colorectal, liver and lung cancer screening as the basis for risk criteria. The official sources I reviewed do not spell out a senior-specific age or eligibility rule, so ask your YAKAP physician whether a referral applies to you.

## Costs: What You Should and Should Not Pay

PhilHealth's member advisory on YAKAP rights (Advisory 2026-0003, signed January 8, 2026) says:

| Clinic type | What you may be charged |
|---|---|
| Public YAKAP clinic | Nothing. Covered consultations, basic laboratory services and other included primary care services must be free. |
| Private YAKAP clinic | Co-payment of up to ₱900 per member per year. After you reach ₱900, remaining covered and necessary YAKAP services for the year should be provided at no additional cost. |

The advisory also says you should not be denied primary care services at your empaneled clinic after completing your FPE and signing your empanelment slip. Clinics also may not reject you if you choose them, unless they have reached the number of beneficiaries they declared they can handle.

## GAMOT: Free Medicines Up to ₱20,000 a Year

GAMOT (Guaranteed and Accessible Medications for Outpatient Treatment) is the medicines part of YAKAP. Key rules from PhilHealth Circular 2025-0013:

- **Annual benefit limit:** ₱20,000 per beneficiary per year. You do not pay out of pocket until that limit is used up.
- **Resets every January 1.** Unused amounts do not carry over.
- **Prescription required:** An authorized physician issues it through the GAMOT App. It carries a Unique Prescription Security Code (UPSC).
- **Where to claim:** Any accredited GAMOT facility. You present the prescription and a government-issued ID.
- **Claiming windows:** Anti-infectives within 2 days of the prescription date, and other medicines within 2 weeks. After that, the pharmacy may still dispense but you pay out of pocket, and the doctor should update the prescription if the medicine is still needed.
- **Maintenance medicines:** A physician may prescribe up to three months, but a facility dispenses up to one month's provision at a time.
- **Prescription validity:** GAMOT prescriptions are automatically cancelled after 90 days from the prescription date.
- **Your receipt:** You get a GAMOT Availment Slip at each dispensing, which shows your remaining benefit balance.

### If someone else picks up your medicines

Many seniors cannot go to the pharmacy. PhilHealth allows an authorized representative to claim on your behalf if they present an authorization letter, the UPSC, and a valid government-issued ID of both the representative and the senior.

### After you use up the ₱20,000

You pay for the medicines yourself at the GAMOT fixed fee (unless you choose a branded medicine priced higher). The circular requires GAMOT facilities to apply existing laws on mandatory discounts and VAT exemption for out-of-pocket payments. Those laws include the Expanded Senior Citizens Act (RA 9994), so present your Senior Citizen ID.

### What GAMOT does not cover

- Medicines used during hospital confinement or outpatient emergency care
- Branded medicines priced above the fixed fee (unless the facility dispenses them at the fixed fee)
- Medicines covered by other outpatient benefits
- Medicines that are not on the GAMOT List

### Which medicines are on the list?

The original GAMOT List in Circular 2025-0013 had 75 medicines across categories such as anti-hypertensives and cardiology, anti-diabetics, cholesterol-lowering drugs, anti-asthma and COPD drugs, antibiotics, and supportive therapy. PhilHealth said revisions to the list will be released through supplemental circulars. Circular 2026-0007 includes an updated table of GAMOT drugs and their indications, and PhilHealth has also issued Circular 2026-0018 on anti-TB drugs under GAMOT. Check PhilHealth's current GAMOT List rather than relying on an old article.

Under Circular 2026-0007, prescriptions must be for a patient's active or current condition. Standby medicines requested "just in case" are not eligible for GAMOT claims, and oral antibiotics cannot be prescribed for more than 14 days per prescription.

PhilHealth also confirmed in a January 2026 advisory that 21 essential medicines under YAKAP remain available at YAKAP-accredited facilities throughout 2026.

## Examples

*These are illustrative examples, not real cases.*

**Example 1: A 68-year-old without a PhilHealth number.** Lola Nena is 68 and has never registered. She brings her Senior Citizen ID, a 1x1 photo and a filled-out PMRF to the LHIO. After receiving her PIN, her daughter helps her choose a nearby clinic in the Member Portal. The clinic schedules her FPE, and she signs the empanelment slip.

**Example 2: A representative picks up medicines.** Mang Ben has a GAMOT prescription from his YAKAP doctor but cannot walk to the pharmacy. His son brings an authorization letter, the UPSC, his own valid ID and a copy of his father's valid ID.

**Example 3: The ₱900 cap.** A private clinic collects ₱600 from a senior over several visits. For the rest of that year, the clinic can charge at most ₱300 more, and then covered services should be free.

## Common Problems

**"The clinic can't find me."** Selection must be processed first. If you selected through an app, confirm that the clinic shows on your Member Portal page. If not, ask your LHIO for help.

**"I want to change clinics."** Empaneled members can only transfer during the fourth quarter (October to December), and the change takes effect in January. Exceptions apply if the clinic closes, loses or has its accreditation suspended or withdrawn, or receives a cease and desist order from the DOH. If you selected a clinic but have not had your FPE, you can transfer at any time through your nearest LHIO.

**"I selected a clinic but never went."** Members who selected a clinic but did not complete an FPE are not retained for the following year. You need to select again.

**"No ID or PhilSys record."** Present a valid government-issued ID or alternative documents for manual validation.

**"The clinic doesn't have my medicines."** News reports in 2026 said some clinics needed time to complete administrative requirements before they could issue prescriptions. GAMOT prescriptions can be claimed at any GAMOT facility, so ask your clinic for a prescription you can take to an accredited pharmacy. A GAMOT facility's refusal to serve a beneficiary is grounds for investigation.

## What to Do If Something Goes Wrong

1. **Charged in a public clinic, or charged beyond ₱900 in a private clinic?** Politely cite PhilHealth Advisory 2026-0003, then report it.
2. **Denied service after completing your FPE and empanelment?** Report it.
3. **Pharmacy refused to serve you?** Report it through PhilHealth's official channels.

PhilHealth's 24/7 Corporate Action Center: (02) 8662-2588 (also listed as (02) 866-225-88). Text lines: Smart 0998-857-2957 or 0968-865-4670, Globe 0917-127-5987 or 0917-110-9812. Email: actioncenter@philhealth.gov.ph or yakaptugon@philhealth.gov.ph.

Keep your PIN, the clinic name, dates of visit, the amount charged, any receipt, and your GAMOT Availment Slip when reporting.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is YAKAP free for senior citizens?

In public YAKAP clinics, covered primary care services must be free. In private clinics, co-payment is capped at ₱900 per member per year. Medicines under GAMOT are free up to ₱20,000 per year.

### Do I need a Senior Citizen ID to use YAKAP?

You need a PhilHealth PIN. For registering at an LHIO, PhilHealth accepts the Senior Citizen ID or any valid government-issued ID.

### Are senior citizens automatically registered in YAKAP?

No. Seniors are covered by PhilHealth by law, but you still need a PIN, then you must choose a clinic, complete the FPE and sign the empanelment slip.

### Can my children select a clinic for me?

PhilHealth rules say beneficiaries aged 18 and above may transact on their own behalf. The official sources reviewed do not describe a general process for a relative to select a clinic for an adult senior, so ask your LHIO what documents they would accept.

### Is YAKAP the same as Konsulta?

YAKAP is an evolution of Konsulta, which PhilHealth has redesigned to include expanded outpatient diagnostics and medicines.

### Does YAKAP pay for hospital confinement?

No. YAKAP is outpatient primary care. Hospital benefits are a separate part of PhilHealth coverage, and PhilHealth explains that inpatient benefits are generally paid to accredited health facilities and deducted from your bill according to applicable rules.

### How many medicines are covered?

The original GAMOT List had 75 medicines, and 21 essential medicines are available at YAKAP clinics. Because the list can be revised, confirm the current list with PhilHealth.

### Can I use any pharmacy?

You can use any accredited GAMOT facility. A regular pharmacy that is not accredited cannot dispense under GAMOT.
